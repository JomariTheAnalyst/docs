You need your own account to use the CMS. The website administrator sends you an invitation by email. After you accept it once, you can open the CMS any time.

## The CMS address

```text
https://www.sixthgearmoto.com/studio
```

Bookmark this address. The page is titled **Sixthgear CMS**.

## First time: accept your invitation

Do this once, on the first day you use the CMS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the invitation email">
    Open the inbox of the email address the administrator invited. Search for `Sanity`. Check **Spam** and **Promotions** too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Accept invitation in the email">
    A Sanity page opens in your browser. Check that the address bar shows `sanity.io`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the account shown on the page">
    It must be the same email address that got the invitation. If it is not, click **Switch accounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in the same way every time">
    If the invited address is a Google account, click **Continue with Google**. Always use the same sign-in method. A different method can create a second account that has no access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Accept invitation">
    Click it once and wait. It can take a minute to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never forward the invitation email, and never accept it with someone else's account. The invitation email never asks for your password. If it does, stop and tell the administrator.
</Warning>

## Every day: open the CMS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the CMS address">
    Open `https://www.sixthgearmoto.com/studio` in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your own account">
    Use the same account and sign-in method you used to accept the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that the CMS opened">
    You see **Sixthgear CMS** at the top and a list on the left with **Blog**, **Homepage Settings**, **Marketing**, **Services Page**, **About Page**, **Services**, and **Popup Ad Campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  If you use more than one Google account in the same browser, open the CMS in a private window. This stops the browser from picking the wrong account.
</Tip>

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The invitation email did not arrive | Check **Spam** and **Promotions**. Then ask the administrator to check the email address and send it again |
| The invitation expired | Ask the administrator to send a new one. Use only the newest email |
| You see "access denied" or the wrong project | Sign out and sign in again with the invited account. If it still fails, ask the administrator |
| You accepted with the wrong account | Stop. Do not edit anything. Ask the administrator to remove that account and invite the right one |
| You can edit but cannot publish | Check the document for red error messages first. If there are none, ask the administrator to check your role |
| The page is blank or keeps loading | Reload once, then try a private window. If it still fails, note the time and any error message, and tell the administrator |

<Info>
  Use only your own account. Never share your password or sign-in codes, and never sign in with another person's account.
</Info>
