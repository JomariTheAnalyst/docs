The website does not update the moment you click **Publish** or **Save**. It keeps a saved copy of each page for a short time so pages load fast. Your change shows once that saved copy is refreshed.

## How long to wait

| What you changed | Where | Usually shows within |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most CMS content, such as the home page hero, pop-ups, promo banners, and rider stories | The CMS | About 1 minute |
| Team, testimonials, store location, the call-to-action banner, and service pages | The CMS | Up to 5 minutes |
| Products, prices, stock, and collections | Shopify admin | Up to about 15 minutes |
| The sitemap, the list of pages Google reads | Updated by the website | About 1 hour |

## If your change does not show

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for the time in the table">
    Do not publish the same change again while you wait.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload the page">
    Reload the website page once. On a phone, close the tab and open the page again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that you published">
    Open the document in the CMS. If you still see a **Publish** button that you can click, the change is not live yet. Click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the web team">
    If the change still does not show after the waiting time, tell the web team. Send the page address, what you changed, and the time you published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A private browser window shows the website the way a new visitor sees it. Use it to check your change.
</Tip>
