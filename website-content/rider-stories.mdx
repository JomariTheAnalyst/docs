Rider stories are the blog posts on the website. In the CMS they are under **Blog**, then **Blog Posts**. Each post gets its own page at `/ph/rider-stories/` followed by the post's page link.

## Write a new rider story

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Blog in the list on the left">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Blog Posts">
    You see all the posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new post">
    Click the button to create a new document at the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Post Title">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate next to Page Link">
    The CMS makes the page link from the title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Short Summary">
    One or two sentences. It shows on the story cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Main Article Image">
    Fill in its **Image Description** too, a short description of the photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Published Date">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the story in Article Content">
    You can add headings, lists, links, and photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    The story shows on the website after about 1 minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Post Title**, **Page Link**, **Short Summary**, **Published Date**, and **Article Content** must be filled in before you can publish.

<Info>
  Leave **Search Result Title**, **Search Result Description**, and **Social Sharing Image** empty. These SEO fields are for the web team.
</Info>

## Which stories show on the home page

The home page shows the 4 newest stories, by **Published Date**. To put a story on the home page, give it the newest date.

<Info>
  The **Show on Homepage** switch does not change anything at the moment. Only the **Published Date** decides which stories show. You can also leave **Blog Category** and **Tags** empty.
</Info>

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Publish** is grey | Fill in every field with a red message |
| The story is not on the home page | Check its **Published Date**. Only the 4 newest show |
| The story page shows "not found" | Wait 1 minute and reload. If it still fails, tell the web team the page link |
