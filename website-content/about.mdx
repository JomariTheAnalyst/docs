The About page is one document in the CMS called **About Page**. Follow [Edit and publish a change](/website-content/edit-and-publish) for the general steps.

## Sections of the About page

**About Page** has seven numbered sections:

{/* The "What it holds" column is based on the section names in the CMS. Check each one against the live page, then remove this comment. */}

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Hero** | The heading and photo at the top |
| **2. Why Choose Us** | The reasons to choose SixthGear |
| **3. Our Story** | The story of SixthGear, in **Story rows** |
| **4. Our Space & Experience** | Cards in **Experience cards**. Up to 8 cards |
| **5. Our Values** | The values of the business |
| **6. CEO Quote** | The quote from the owner |
| **7. CTA Banner** | The banner at the bottom |

Each section has its own **Use Sanity content** switch. It must be on for your changes to show.

## The about section on the home page

The home page has its own short about section. It is not part of **About Page**. Change it in **Homepage Settings**. See [Change the home page](/website-content/home-page).
