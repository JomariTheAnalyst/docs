Pop-ups and banners are for promotions and sales. They live in two places in the CMS:

| What | Where in the CMS |
| --- | --- |
| The pop-up itself | **Popup Ad Campaign** |
| Which pop-up is live | **Marketing**, field **Active Popup Campaign** |
| Promo banners | **Marketing**, field **Promo Wide Banners** |
| Featured collection banners | **Marketing**, field **Featured Collection Banners** |
| The announcement bar | **Marketing**, field **Announcement Bar** |

The person who manages pop-ups and promo banners also manages the announcement bar.

Follow [Edit and publish a change](/website-content/edit-and-publish) for the general steps. Changes here show within about 1 minute.

## Show a pop-up

A pop-up needs two steps. First you make the pop-up. Then you choose it in **Marketing**.

### Make the pop-up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Popup Ad Campaign in the list on the left">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new campaign">
    Click the button to create a new document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Name (Internal)">
    Only staff see this name. For example, "Holiday sale December."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Popup Image">
    Always add an image. The CMS marks it as required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Start Date and End Date">
    The pop-up shows only between these dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Image Link">
    This is the page customers go to when they click the image, for example a collection page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Enabled">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Heading (Optional)**, **Button Label (Optional)**, and **Button Link (Optional)** add text and a button on top of the image. **Trigger Delay (seconds)** sets how long the website waits before it shows the pop-up.

### Make it live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Marketing in the list on the left">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the campaign in Active Popup Campaign">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Open the home page in a private window after about 1 minute. The pop-up appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Stop a pop-up

Open **Marketing**, clear **Active Popup Campaign**, and click **Publish**. You can also turn off **Enabled** in the campaign and publish it.

## Change the announcement bar

The announcement bar is the thin strip of short messages at the very top of the website. The messages scroll one after another.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Marketing in the list on the left">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Announcement Bar">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a message, or click the one to change">
    Open **Messages** to see them all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the Message Text">
    Keep it to 60 characters or fewer, for example "Free store pickup in about 4 hours."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a link if you need one">
    Fill in **Link (optional)** with the page customers go to when they click the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Active for the message">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that Show Announcement Bar is on">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Check the top of the website after about 1 minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show Announcement Bar** | Turns the whole bar on or off |
| **Background Color** | Orange, Black, or White |
| **Marquee Pace (seconds per message)** | How long each message stays. From 2 to 20 seconds |
| **Active** on a message | Turn it off to hide one message without deleting it |

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The pop-up does not show | Check that it is chosen in **Active Popup Campaign**, that **Enabled** is on, and that today is between the start and end dates |
| A banner does not show | Check that **Active** is on and that you clicked **Publish** |
| The announcement bar does not show | Check that **Show Announcement Bar** is on and that at least one message has **Active** on |
