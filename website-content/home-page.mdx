Most of the home page is in one CMS document called **Homepage Settings**. It has many sections, one for each part of the page. Follow [Edit and publish a change](/website-content/edit-and-publish) for the general steps.

<Info>
  The section names in the CMS include numbers, such as "About Section on Homepage - 4th section." These numbers do not match the order on the live website. Find the section by its name, not its number.
</Info>

## Sections you can change

| On the website | Section name in Homepage Settings | Main fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The big banner at the top | **First Section of Homepage - Main Banner** | **Main banner title**, **Short supporting text**, **Main button text**, **Main button link**, **Banner background images** |
| The video | **Featured Video on Homepage - 3rd section** | **Video source**, **Video upload** or **Cloudinary or YouTube URL**, **Poster image**, **Video title**, **Video description** |
| The about section | **About Section on Homepage - 4th section** | **Main heading**, **About text**, **Short highlight points**, **Top photo**, **Bottom photo** |
| Product categories | **Product Categories Section on Homepage - 5th section** | **Main heading**, **Category cards in this section** |
| The services list | **Motorcycle Services Section on Homepage - 8th section** | **Main heading**, **Service cards** |
| The team | **Meet the Team Section - 13th section** | **Main heading**, **Team member cards** |
| Customer reviews | **Customer Reviews Section - 14th section** | **Main heading**, **Review cards** |
| Store location | **Store Location and Contact Section - 15th section** | **Store name**, **Address**, **Phone number**, **Opening hours**, **Directions button link** |
| The last banner, the call to action | **Final Call to Action Banner - 16th section** | **Main banner message**, **Words to highlight in orange**, **Button text**, **Button link**, **Social media links** |

Each section has a switch at the top, such as **Use Sanity content**. It must be on for your changes to show. See [The "Use Sanity content" switch](/website-content/edit-and-publish#the-use-sanity-content-switch).

## Change the big banner at the top

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Homepage Settings">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open First Section of Homepage - Main Banner">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the title, text, or buttons">
    **Main banner title**, **Main button text**, and **Main button link** must always be filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the photos in Banner background images">
    Each photo needs an **Image Alt Text**, a short description of the photo. If the photo looks wrong on a phone, add a **Mobile Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Check the home page after about 1 minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If **Banner background images** is empty, the website shows its built-in banner photos.
</Tip>

## Tips for each section

| Section | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| Video | The **Cloudinary or YouTube URL** field only accepts Cloudinary or YouTube links |
| Team | Keep **Short description** to 15 words or fewer. Use **Display order** to set the order, and **Show on homepage** to hide a person without deleting them |
| Customer reviews | Do not add quotation marks in **Customer review**. The website adds them |
| Store location | **Address** changes the words only, not the pin on the map. It also does not change the Contact page or the footer. See [Known issue: store location](/website-content/web-team-only#known-issue-store-location) |
| Call to action | The Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok links must all be filled in |

## Rider stories on the home page

The home page shows the 4 newest blog posts, by **Published Date**. You do not choose them in Homepage Settings. See [Rider stories](/website-content/rider-stories).

## Sections to leave alone

These sections are in Homepage Settings, but the website does not show them. Changes to them do nothing.

- **Scrolling Marquee Below Main Banner**. The marquee strip is set by the web team. See [Things only the web team can change](/website-content/web-team-only).
- **Brands Section on Homepage - 2nd section**. The brand carousel comes from Shopify collections.
- **Coffee Section on Homepage - 7th section**
- **What We Offer - 9th section**
- **Motorcycle Brands We Service - 10th section**
- **Customer Photos Section - 11th section**
- **Franchise Invitation Section - 12th section**

If you need one of these on the website, ask the web team.
