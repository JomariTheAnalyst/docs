The Services part of the website has two kinds of pages. Each one has its own place in the CMS.

| Page on the website | Where in the CMS |
| --- | --- |
| The main Services page | **Services Page** |
| The page for one service | **Services**, then the service |

Follow [Edit and publish a change](/website-content/edit-and-publish) for the general steps. Service changes can take up to 5 minutes to show.

## The main Services page

**Services Page** has seven numbered sections:

{/* The "What it holds" column is based on the section names in the CMS. Check each one against the live page, then remove this comment. */}

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Hero** | The heading, description, and background photo at the top |
| **2. Expertise & Assistance** | The text about the team's skills |
| **3. Brands We Service** | The motorcycle brands the workshop services |
| **4. Services Grid** | Which services show, and in what order |
| **5. Process of Work** | The steps of a service visit |
| **6. Services Gallery** | Photos of the workshop |
| **7. CTA Banner** | The banner at the bottom |

Each section has its own **Use Sanity content** switch. It must be on for your changes to show.

## What you can change and what the web team changes

| You can | Only the web team can |
| --- | --- |
| Change the text of an existing service | Add a new service |
| Change the photos of an existing service | Change **SEO Title**, **SEO Description**, and **Social Share Image** |
| Change the main **Services Page** | Change **Page URL** |

## Change one service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Services in the list on the left">
    You see a list of all services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the service">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the words or photo">
    The fields you will use most are **Service Name**, **Short Description**, **Full Description**, **Service Page Photo**, **What This Service Includes**, and **Frequently Asked Questions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Check the service page after up to 5 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never change **Page URL** after a service is published. It is the service's web address. Changing it breaks every link to that page, including links in Google.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Keep **Short Description** under 120 characters. It shows on the service cards.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Leave **SEO Title**, **SEO Description**, and **Social Share Image** to the web team. They control how the page looks in Google and when shared on social media. To add a new service, ask the web team.
</Info>

## Services on the home page

The services list on the home page is separate. Change it in **Homepage Settings**. See [Change the home page](/website-content/home-page).
