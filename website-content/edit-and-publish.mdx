Every change in the CMS follows the same four steps: open the document, edit it, publish it, and check the website. A document is one item in the CMS, for example **Homepage Settings** or one blog post.

## Before you start

- You can open the CMS. See [Open the CMS](/website-content/open-the-cms).
- You have the exact new words, photos, and links, and your supervisor or the owner approved them.
- The part you want to change is managed in the CMS. See [What you can change on the website](/website-content/overview).

## Edit and publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the document">
    In the list on the left, click the area, for example **Homepage Settings**. For lists such as **Blog Posts**, click the item you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the section">
    Scroll to the section you want to change. Click it to open its fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your change">
    Change only the fields in your request. The CMS saves your work as a draft as you type. A draft is a change that is not on the website yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix any red messages">
    A red message means a field is missing or too long. Fix it before you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    The button is at the bottom right. Click it once and wait until it finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the live website">
    Wait for the time in [When your change will show](/website-content/when-changes-show). Then open the page on a computer and on a phone and check your change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your change is not on the website until you click **Publish**. Do not publish a test change just to see if it works.
</Warning>

## The "Use Sanity content" switch

Most sections have a switch at the top, such as **Use Sanity content** or **Use this Services section**.

| Switch | What the website shows |
| --- | --- |
| On | The words and photos you enter in the CMS |
| Off | The website's built-in content. Your CMS changes do not show |

When the switch is on, every field in that section must be filled in before you can publish. If a change does not show, check this switch first.

## Photos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the approved photo">
    Use a clear photo that SixthGear has permission to use. Do not use a small photo that looks blurry when made bigger.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it to the photo field">
    Click the photo field and upload the file, or pick a photo that is already in the CMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the focus point">
    Click the crop button and move the focus point onto the most important part of the photo. The website crops photos differently on computers and phones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the photo description">
    Describe what the photo shows in one short sentence, for example "Mechanic changing the oil on a sport bike." Screen readers and Google use this text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Some photos have a separate **Mobile Image** field. Use it when the main photo does not look right on a phone.

<Warning>
  Never upload photos that show customer details, receipts, order screens, passwords, or account pages.
</Warning>

## Preview before you publish

The **Visual Editor** at the top of the CMS shows your draft on a copy of the website before you publish. It works for the home page, the About page, the Services page, and blog posts.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Publish** button is grey | Look for red messages and fix them. If there are none, ask the administrator to check your role |
| The website still shows the old content | Wait for the time in [When your change will show](/website-content/when-changes-show). Then check that the section's switch is on |
| A different part of the website changed | Stop. Do not publish again. Tell the web team which document and field you changed |
| You published a mistake | Change the field back to the old text and publish again. Tell your supervisor |
