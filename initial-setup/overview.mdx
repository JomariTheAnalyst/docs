<Info>

</Info>

## What each role normally needs

| Role | Main access needed |
| --- | --- |
| Management | Documentation, approved Shopify reports or settings, and the company access records |
| Store or ecommerce staff | Shopify products, orders, inventory, and the approved POS tools |
| Content editor | SixthGear CMS and the live website pages they are responsible for |
| Developer | Source code, local setup, approved hosting access, and the required test tools |

## Complete the setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the person's role">
    Record what work the person is expected to do before any access is granted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the approved company email">
    Sign in or accept invitations using the account approved for that person and role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the correct company system">
    Confirm the SixthGear store, CMS workspace, repository, or other company account before making changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up account protection">
    Complete MFA using the approved method and keep recovery information in secure company storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test access without changing live information">
    Confirm that the required pages and tools open. Do not publish test content, change prices, or edit live settings only to prove access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record missing access">
    If something is unavailable, record the missing area and ask the authorized administrator to review the assigned role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not use another employee's account, save a company password on a personal device, or copy passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, API keys, or bank details into Mintlify, email, or chat.
</Warning>

## Setup is complete when

- The correct SixthGear systems open.
- The person has only the access needed for their work.
- A named account is used where available.
- MFA and recovery responsibilities are clear.
- No secret information is stored in the documentation.
- The person knows where to report an access problem.

<Check>
  A successful setup gives the person enough access to complete approved work without giving unnecessary control over billing, users, payments, security, or company ownership.
</Check>
