Once an order is paid and every item is on the shelf, you can prepare it. A packing slip is a printed list of the items in the order. You use it to pick the items and check the package.

## Before you start

- The order is marked **Paid**. See [Check new orders](/online-orders/check-new-orders).
- Every item is on the shelf.

## Pick and pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order in Shopify admin">
    Go to **Orders** and click the SG order number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print the packing slip">
    Click **Print packing slip**. The slip shows the order number and every item, size, and quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick each item from the shelf">
    Take the exact item, size, and color on the slip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the items against the slip">
    Tick each line on the slip as you check it. Count the quantities again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pack the items">
    Put the items and the packing slip in the package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the order number on the package">
    Write the SG number clearly on the outside, for example **SG1001**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Pack one order at a time. Finish and label it before you start the next one, so items do not get mixed up.
</Tip>

Next, hand the package over. See [Complete an order](/online-orders/complete-an-order).

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The item on the shelf is damaged | Do not pack it. Call your supervisor |
| The size or color on the slip is not on the shelf | Call your supervisor before you contact the customer |
| The packing slip did not print | Check the printer. You can also pick the items from the order screen and write the order number by hand |
