New online orders wait in Shopify until someone prepares them. Check for them during the day so customers get their orders on time.

## Find the orders that are waiting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Shopify admin">
    Use the store computer or tablet and log in with your own account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Orders">
    You see the list of all orders, newest at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Unfulfilled filter">
    The list now shows only orders that have not been handed over yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check each order

Do these checks for every order in the list, starting with the oldest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the payment status">
    Only prepare orders marked **Paid**. If an order shows anything other than **Paid**, do not prepare it. Call your supervisor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    Click the SG order number. You see the items, sizes, quantities, and the customer's delivery choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for warnings">
    If the order shows that it was cancelled, is on hold, or has a risk warning from Shopify, do not prepare it. Call your supervisor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the customer's notes">
    Look for a note or delivery instructions from the customer on the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the delivery method">
    See whether the customer chose shipping or store pickup. Store pickup shows as **Shop location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that every item is on the shelf">
    Look for each item, in the right size and color, and the right quantity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If an item is missing or damaged, call your supervisor before you contact the customer. Do not promise the customer a replacement, a refund, or a new date.
</Warning>

When all checks pass, go to [Pick and pack an order](/online-orders/prepare-an-order).

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The order is not marked **Paid** | Do not prepare it. Call your supervisor |
| An item is not on the shelf | Call your supervisor before you contact the customer |
| The Shopify stock count says the item is there, but you cannot find it | Call your supervisor. Do not change the stock count yourself |
| You cannot see the **Orders** page | Your account may not have access. Ask your supervisor |
