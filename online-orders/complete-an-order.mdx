Shopify does not mark orders as done on its own. You mark every order yourself, after the package has left the store. Follow the section that matches the order's delivery method: shipping or store pickup.

<Warning>
  Only mark an order as done after you have handed the package to the courier or to the customer. Marking it early tells the customer their order is on the way when it is not.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Never mark the whole order as done when an item is still missing or not yet handed over. Call your supervisor instead.
</Warning>

## Shipping

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hand the package to the courier">
    Keep the courier's receipt or waybill. It has the tracking number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the order in Shopify admin">
    Go to **Orders** and click the SG order number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Fulfill items">
    You see the items in the order and fields for the tracking details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the courier">
    Choose or type the courier's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the tracking number">
    Copy it exactly from the courier's receipt or waybill. Check that it is the tracking number for this package, not another order's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the box to notify the customer">
    The customer then gets an email with the tracking number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Fulfill items**. The order now shows **Fulfilled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Store pickup

### When the package is ready

<Steps>
  <Step title="Put the package in the pickup area">
    Keep it where staff at the counter can find it by the SG number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the order in Shopify admin">
    Go to **Orders** and click the SG order number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Mark as ready for pickup">
    Shopify tells the customer that the order is ready to collect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When the customer arrives

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for their name and order number">
    Check that both match the order in Shopify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the package">
    Let the customer check the items if they want to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Mark as picked up">
    The order now shows **Fulfilled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You marked an order as done by mistake | Tell your supervisor right away |
| You typed the wrong tracking number | Tell your supervisor. They can correct it on the order |
| The name or order number does not match at pickup | Do not hand over the package. Call your supervisor |
| The customer did not come to pick up the order | Keep the package in the pickup area and tell your supervisor |
