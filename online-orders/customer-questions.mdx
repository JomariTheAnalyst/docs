Customers may call, message, or visit to ask about their online order. You can tell them where the order is. Anything more than that goes to your supervisor.

## Find the order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for the SG order number">
    If the customer does not have it, ask for the name or email address on the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Orders in Shopify admin">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the order">
    Type the SG number, the customer's name, or their email address in the search box at the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    Check that the name on the order matches the customer you are talking to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can share

| You can share | You must never share |
| --- | --- |
| Whether the order is paid | Another customer's name, address, phone, email, or order |
| Whether the order is being prepared, ready for pickup, or shipped | Payment details, such as card numbers or account names |
| The courier's tracking number | Anything about the order before you are sure you are talking to the right customer |

<Warning>
  If you are not sure the person is the customer who placed the order, do not share anything. Call your supervisor.
</Warning>

## When to call your supervisor

Call your supervisor for:

- A request to cancel the order
- A request for a refund, return, or exchange
- A complaint about an item, a delay, or the courier
- Any question you cannot answer from the order screen

Tell the customer that your supervisor will get back to them. Do not promise a refund, a replacement, or a date.
