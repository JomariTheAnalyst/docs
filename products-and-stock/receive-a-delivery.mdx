When new stock arrives from a supplier, add it to Shopify right away. The website and the counter tablet both use this stock count. If a delivery is not added, the website may show an item as sold out when it is actually on the shelf.

## Before you add the stock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Count what arrived">
    Count every item and size in the delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare with the supplier's delivery receipt">
    If the count does not match the receipt, write the difference on the receipt and tell your supervisor before adding anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for new products">
    If an item is not in Shopify yet, add it first. See [Add a website product](/shopify/products/add-product).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add the stock in Shopify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the inventory list">
    In Shopify admin, go to **Products** and click **Inventory**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Search by name or SKU. Each size or color has its own row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the quantity that arrived">
    Click the **On hand** number for that row. Choose to adjust by an amount, enter how many arrived, and choose the reason **Received**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Repeat for every item and size in the delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add the quantity that arrived, not the new total. If you had 3 and 5 arrived, enter 5. Shopify adds it to make 8.
</Tip>

## After adding the stock

- Keep the supplier's delivery receipt with the day's papers.
- During the trial period, add the same delivery in Loyverse too.
- Put the items on the shelf and check a few of them on the website.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You entered the new total instead of the quantity that arrived | Adjust the same row again by the extra amount, with the reason **Correction** |
| The item has no row in the inventory list | Its stock is not tracked. Open the product and turn on **Track quantity** |
| A large delivery has many items | Ask the person in charge of the product list to add it using a spreadsheet import |
