This section is for the people who add and update products in Shopify admin. Shopify admin is the website where you manage the store, at the address that ends in `myshopify.com`. It is different from the Shopify POS app on the counter tablet.

## One place for every product

Shopify is the only place where products, prices, and stock are kept. The website and the counter tablet both read from it. When you change a price or the stock in Shopify, the website and the tablet both show the change.

<Warning>
  During the trial period, Loyverse still has its own copy of products and prices. If you change a price in Shopify, change it in Loyverse too, so both systems charge the same amount.
</Warning>

## Two kinds of products

| Kind | Examples | Where it is sold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website products | Helmets, riding gear, parts, accessories | On the website and at the counter |
| Counter-only items | Services, carwash, hauling, café drinks and food | At the counter only. Never on the website |

The setting that decides this is called **Sales channels**. A website product has both the website's sales channel and **Point of Sale** turned on. A counter-only item has only **Point of Sale** turned on.

## Every product needs two labels

Every product has two labels at the bottom of its page in Shopify admin: **Business unit** and **Report category**. The daily sales report uses them to know where each sale belongs. A product without both labels shows up in the report as "Needs fixing."

The labels only accept exact spelling. If you type something that is not on the list, Shopify will not save the product.

| Business unit | Report categories |
| --- | --- |
| `Retail` | `Riding Gear Sales`, `Helmet Sales`, `Apparel Sales`, `Intercom & Other Accessories`, `Electronics and Auxiliary Lights Sales`, `Luggage Systems`, `Tires / Performance Parts`, `Lubricants / Consumables`, `Motorcycle Accessories Sales`, `Motorcycle Exhaust Sales`, `Other Retail Sales` |
| `Service Department` | `PMS / Maintenance Labor`, `Installation Labor`, `Diagnostic Labor`, `Electrical Repairs`, `Tire Services`, `Performance Upgrades`, `Other Labor Revenue` |
| `Carwash & Detailing` | `Motowash`, `Carwash`, `Motorcycle Detailing`, `Car Detailing`, `Ceramic / Coating Services`, `Engine Detailing`, `Other Detailing Services` |
| `Sixth Gear Cafe` | `Coffee Sales`, `Non-Coffee Beverages`, `Food / Snacks`, `Merchandise`, `Other Cafe Revenue` |
| `Bike Hauling & Towing` | `Local Hauling`, `Long Distance Hauling`, `Towing Services`, `Event Logistics`, `Other Hauling Revenue` |

<Tip>
  Write "Cafe" without the accent in the labels. The report adds the accent when it prints.
</Tip>

## Fields that change what the website shows

These fields matter for website products. Use the spelling that already exists in Shopify. A second spelling of the same brand or type shows up on the website as a separate filter.

| Field | What it does on the website |
| --- | --- |
| **Vendor** | The brand. Used for filters and "You may like" suggestions |
| **Product type** | Used for the filters on the shop page |
| **Collections** | Groups products into pages, such as brand pages |
| Tag `featured` | Shows the product in the Featured section of the home page |
| Tags `hot-deal` and `new-arrival` | Show a badge on the product card |
| **Compare-at price** | Shows the item as on sale, with the old price crossed out |

## Product status

| Status | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | The product is not finished yet. Customers and staff cannot buy it |
| **Active** | The product is finished and ready to sell |
| **Archived** | The product is no longer sold. Its past sales history is kept |

<Warning>
  Never delete a product that has been sold before. Archive it instead, so past sales keep their details.
</Warning>
