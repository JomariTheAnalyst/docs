A price change in Shopify updates the website and the counter tablet at the same time. Only change prices that have been approved by the owner or your supervisor.

## Change the price of one item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    In Shopify admin, go to **Products** and click the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the right size or color">
    If the product has sizes or colors, click the one you want to change. Each can have its own price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new price">
    Type the new price in **Price**. Never set a website product to ₱0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Put an item on sale

To show an item as on sale on the website, use two prices:

| Field | What to enter | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | The new, lower price the customer pays | ₱7,500 |
| **Compare-at price** | The old, regular price | ₱8,400 |

The website then shows the old price crossed out and the percentage saved. When the sale ends, put the regular price back in **Price** and clear **Compare-at price**.

<Warning>
  Only use **Compare-at price** for a real sale. Showing a fake "old price" misleads customers.
</Warning>

## Change many prices at once

To change prices for many items, select them in the **Products** list and click **Bulk edit**. A table opens where you can change several prices, then click **Save**. Check a few of them afterward.

## Check that it worked

1. On the counter tablet, search for the item and check the new price.
2. After about 15 minutes, check the item on the website.
3. During the trial period, change the same price in Loyverse too.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The website still shows the old price | Wait 15 minutes and refresh the page |
| The tablet still shows the old price | Close and reopen the Shopify POS app |
| The item does not show as on sale | Check that **Compare-at price** is higher than **Price** |
