Use this page to add an item that is sold only at the counter: a service, a carwash package, a hauling job, or a café drink or food item. These items never appear on the website.

## Choose how the item is priced

| How the price works | Example | How to set it up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One fixed price | Oat Milk | One product with one price |
| A choice of fixed prices | Carwash (package, then size), hauling destinations, café drink sizes | One product with options. Each choice has its own price |
| Price changes every time | Other Detailing, Towing Services | One product priced at ₱1. Staff enter the amount as the quantity at the counter |

Only add options when the choices really have different prices. If there is one price, do not add options.

## Add the item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add product">
    In Shopify admin, go to **Products** and click **Add product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a clear title">
    Use the name staff will search for at the counter, for example "Oat Milk" or "Spanish Latte." A photo is not needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the price">
    Enter the fixed price. For an item where the price changes every time, enter **₱1**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add options only if needed">
    For a choice of prices, add an option for each choice. Carwash has two: first the package, then the size (S, M, L, XL, XXL for cars, MC-S, MC-M, MC-L for motorcycles). Enter the price of each combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off stock tracking for services">
    For services, carwash, and hauling, turn off **Track quantity**. Labor has no stock. For café items, ask your supervisor whether the item's stock is counted, for example bottled drinks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag it pos-only">
    In **Tags**, add `pos-only`. Every counter-only item has this tag, so they are easy to find and check later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the two labels">
    At the bottom of the page, fill in **Business unit** and **Report category**. See the list in [How products work](/products-and-stock/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Point of Sale only">
    In **Sales channels**, turn on **Point of Sale** and turn off the website's sales channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status to Active and save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Double-check **Sales channels** before you save. If the website's sales channel is left on, the service or café item shows up on the website for online customers.
</Warning>

## Check that it worked

1. On the counter tablet, search for the item. Check the price and options.
2. Search for the item on the website. It must not appear.
3. During the trial period, add the same item to Loyverse with the same price.

## After any import

An import can turn the website's sales channel back on for counter-only items. After every import:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Products in Shopify admin">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list by the tag pos-only">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the sales channels of every item in the list">
    Each one must show **Point of Sale** only. Turn off the website's sales channel on any that show it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Before you import](/shopify/bulk-import#before-you-import).

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The item appears on the website | Open the item, turn off the website's sales channel, and save. It disappears from the website within about 15 minutes |
| Staff cannot find the item on the tablet | Check that **Point of Sale** is on and the status is **Active** |
| Shopify will not save the item | Check the spelling of the two labels |
