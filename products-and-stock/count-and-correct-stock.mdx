The stock number in Shopify should always match what is on the shelf. Correct it when you do a stock count, or when an item is damaged, lost, or used for the store.

## When to correct stock

| Situation | Reason to choose in Shopify |
| --- | --- |
| You counted the shelf and the number is different | **Count** |
| An item is damaged and cannot be sold | **Damaged** |
| An item is missing or stolen | **Theft or loss** |
| An item was given away or used for a promotion | **Promotion or donation** |
| You made a mistake in an earlier change | **Correction** |

<Warning>
  Get your supervisor's approval before lowering stock for damage, loss, or a giveaway. These changes mean the store lost money, so they need to be checked.
</Warning>

## Correct the stock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Count the item on the shelf">
    Count every size or color separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the inventory list">
    In Shopify admin, go to **Products** and click **Inventory**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Search by name or SKU. Each size or color has its own row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the correct number">
    Click the **On hand** number. Enter the new quantity you counted, and choose the reason from the table above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Doing a full stock count

For a full count of the store, count during quiet hours or after closing. Items sold while you are counting will make the numbers wrong. Count one section at a time and correct each section before moving to the next.

During the trial period, correct the same items in Loyverse too.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The number keeps being wrong for the same item | Tell your supervisor. Sales may be going into Loyverse but not Shopify |
| You corrected the wrong size or color | Correct both rows again, with the reason **Correction** |
| The item shows as sold out on the website but is on the shelf | Count it and correct the stock. The website updates within about 15 minutes |
