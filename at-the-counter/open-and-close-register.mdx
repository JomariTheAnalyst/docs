Every day starts with opening the register and ends with closing it. Opening records how much cash is in the drawer at the start. Closing checks that the cash in the drawer matches the cash sales of the day.

## Open the register

Do this before the first customer of the day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the tablet and the printer">
    Check that the printer has paper and that its light is steady, not blinking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Shopify POS and enter your PIN">
    Use your own PIN. Every sale is saved under the PIN that was used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Count the starting cash">
    Count the cash your supervisor put in the drawer for change. This is called the starting cash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the cash session">
    Open the cash drawer screen in Shopify POS, start a new session, and enter the starting cash you counted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Loyverse too">
    During the trial period, open Loyverse the same way you do today. See [Using Loyverse and Shopify together](/at-the-counter/loyverse-and-shopify).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cash that leaves or enters the drawer during the day

Sometimes cash leaves the drawer for a reason that is not a sale, for example buying supplies or a staff cash advance. Record it every time, or the drawer will not match at closing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your supervisor's approval first">
    Never take cash out of the drawer without approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record it on the cash drawer screen">
    Choose to add or remove cash, enter the exact amount, and write the reason. For example: "Staff cash advance, approved by supervisor" or "Bought ice for the café."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the receipt">
    If you bought something, put the receipt with the day's papers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Close the register

Do this after the last customer of the day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Count all the cash in the drawer">
    Count it twice. Include the starting cash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the cash session">
    On the cash drawer screen, close the session and enter the amount you counted. Shopify shows the amount it expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the difference">
    If your count and the expected amount are the same, you are done with the cash. If they are different, count again. If they are still different, write a note explaining what you know and tell your supervisor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather the day's papers">
    Put together the card terminal slips, receipts for cash taken out, and any Shopify receipts that were printed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close Loyverse">
    Close Loyverse the same way you do today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charge the tablet">
    Leave the tablet on its charger overnight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your supervisor checks after closing

| Check | Should match |
| --- | --- |
| Cash counted in the drawer | Expected cash in Shopify |
| Card terminal slips | **Card Terminal** total in Shopify |
| Payments received in the store's QR PH app | **QR PH** total in Shopify |
| Loyverse total for the day | Shopify daily sales report total |

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The cash is short or over | Count again. If it is still different, write a note and tell your supervisor. Do not add or remove your own money to make it match |
| You forgot to record cash taken out earlier | Tell your supervisor. They will record it with the correct reason |
| The tablet died during the day | Keep selling in Loyverse, charge the tablet, and tell your supervisor which sales still need to be entered in Shopify |
