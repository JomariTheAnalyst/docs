Services, carwash, and hauling are sold at the counter only. They never appear on the website. Each item already belongs to the right business unit and category, so you only need to pick the right item and choices.

| Kind | Examples | How you sell it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose options | Carwash and detailing, Local Hauling, Long Distance Hauling | Pick the package and size, or the destination |
| Per panel or per mag | Panel buffing, mags detailing | The quantity is the number of panels or mags |
| Price changes every time | Other Detailing, Towing Services, Event Logistics, Other Hauling | Enter the amount as the quantity |

### Panel buffing and mags detailing

These are priced per panel and per mag. Enter the number of panels or mags as the quantity. For example, detailing 4 mags is quantity **4**.

### Jobs not on the chart

Use **Other Detailing**. It costs ₱1, and you enter the amount as the quantity. See [Price changes every time](#price-changes-every-time).

### Destinations with a price range

Some destinations have a price range: **Pangasinan**, **Ilocos Sur**, and **Ilocos Norte**. In Shopify, these are set at the highest price of the range. If the agreed price is lower, ask your supervisor to lower the price before checkout. Shopify can lower a price but cannot raise it.

## Price changes every time

**Other Detailing**, **Towing Services**, **Event Logistics**, and **Other Hauling** cost ₱1 each in Shopify. You enter the amount the customer will pay as the quantity. For example, a tow that costs ₱850 is quantity **850**. The cart then shows ₱850.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the final amount">
    Get it from the person in charge of the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the item and add it">
    For example, search "towing."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the item in the cart and change the quantity to the amount">
    For ₱850, type **850**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line total">
    The line total must match the amount the customer will pay. If it does not, fix the quantity before you tap **Checkout**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot find the item | Ask your supervisor. Do not use **Custom sale** |
| You are not sure of the vehicle's size | Ask your supervisor |
| The line total is wrong after entering the quantity | Tap the item and correct the quantity before checkout |
| The customer's destination is not on the hauling list | Ask your supervisor for the price and which item to use |
