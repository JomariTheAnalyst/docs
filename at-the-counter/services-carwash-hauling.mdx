Services, carwash, and hauling are sold at the counter only. They never appear on the website. Like every other item, each one already belongs to the right business unit and category, so you only need to pick the correct item.

There are three kinds of service items.

| Kind | Examples | How you sell it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One fixed price | Motowash, Brake Fluid Flushing | Add it to the cart |
| A choice of fixed prices | Carwash sizes, hauling destinations | Choose the right option |
| Price changes every time | Electrical Repairs, Installation Labor, Towing Services | Enter the amount as the quantity |

## Services with one fixed price

Search for the service and add it to the cart, the same way as any item. See [Ring up a sale](/at-the-counter/ring-up-a-sale).

## Services with a choice of prices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the service">
    For example, search "carwash" or "hauling."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right option">
    For **Carwash**, choose the size: Carwash Small, Carwash Medium, Carwash Large, or Carwash XL. For **Local Hauling** and **Long Distance Hauling**, choose the destination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the price">
    The price should match the price list for that size or destination.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Hauling destinations with a price range

Some destinations have a price range instead of one price: **Pangasinan**, **Ilocos Sur**, and **Ilocos Norte**. In Shopify, these are set at the highest price of the range. If the agreed price is lower, ask your supervisor to lower the price on that item before checkout. Shopify can lower a price but cannot raise it.

## Services where the price changes every time

These services cost ₱1 each in Shopify. You enter the amount the customer will pay as the quantity. For example, an electrical repair that costs ₱850 is entered as quantity **850**. The cart then shows ₱850.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the final amount">
    Get the final amount from the mechanic or the person in charge of the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the service and add it">
    For example, search "electrical."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the item in the cart and change the quantity to the amount">
    For ₱850, type **850**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line total">
    The line total must match the amount the customer will pay. If it does not, fix the quantity before you tap **Checkout**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always use the real service item, even when the price changes. Do not use **Custom sale**. A custom sale has no category, so the service would be missing from the daily sales report.
</Warning>

## Carwash add-ons

**Disinfectant** and **Mr. Cool** are sold like normal items. Choose the scent the customer wants.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot find the service | Ask your supervisor. Do not use **Custom sale** |
| The line total is wrong after entering the quantity | Tap the item and correct the quantity before checkout |
| The customer's destination is not on the hauling list | Ask your supervisor for the price and which item to use |
