Only supervisors do refunds and returns. Staff should call you instead of doing them. During the trial period, every refund must be done in both Shopify and Loyverse.

## Before you refund

- Find the original receipt or ask for the date and what was bought.
- Check the item. Decide if it can go back on the shelf or not.
- Decide how the money goes back to the customer. It should go back the same way they paid.

## Do a refund in Shopify POS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the original order">
    Open the orders list in Shopify POS and search by receipt number, date, or customer name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a return">
    Choose the items being returned and how many.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether to restock">
    Restock only if the item can be sold again. Do not restock services, café items, or damaged goods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the money back">
    Refund to the same payment method the customer used. For cash, give the cash from the drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the reason">
    Write a short reason, for example "Wrong helmet size, customer returned it unused."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the same refund in Loyverse">
    Use the same items and amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How money goes back

| Customer paid with | How to give it back |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | Cash from the drawer. Shopify records it, so the drawer still matches at closing |
| QR PH | Send the money back through the store's QR PH or e-wallet app, then record the refund in Shopify |
| Card Terminal | Reverse it on the bank's card terminal, then record the refund in Shopify |
| Care of Boss | Nothing to give back. Ask the owner before cancelling |

## Exchanges

An exchange is a return plus a new sale. Refund the returned item first, then ring up the new item as a normal sale. If the new item costs more, the customer pays the difference.

## How refunds show in the daily sales report

A refund is subtracted from its category on the day you do the refund, not on the day of the original sale. It also appears in the report's list of refunds for that day.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot find the original order | Search by date and item instead of receipt number. If it was sold only in Loyverse, refund it in Loyverse and tell the owner |
| The customer wants a refund to a different payment method | Ask the owner before doing it |
| You restocked an item by mistake | Adjust the stock in Shopify admin, or ask the person in charge of inventory |
