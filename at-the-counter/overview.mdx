This section is for everyone who takes payments at SixthGear: retail, the service desk, the carwash, the café, and hauling. It shows how to ring up sales on the store tablet using Shopify POS.

POS means point of sale. It is the app you use to record what the customer bought and how they paid.

## What you use at the counter

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Samsung tablet with the **Shopify POS** app | Ringing up every sale |
| Epson receipt printer | Printing receipts |
| Bank card terminal | Charging credit and debit cards. Shopify only records the payment, it does not charge the card |
| QR PH code | Letting customers pay with GCash, Maya, or their bank app |
| Loyverse | Still used during the trial period. See [Using Loyverse and Shopify together](/at-the-counter/loyverse-and-shopify) |

## Before your first shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your own staff PIN">
    Ask your supervisor for your personal PIN. Every sale is saved under the PIN that was used, so never use another person's PIN and never share yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the two main pages">
    Read [Using Loyverse and Shopify together](/at-the-counter/loyverse-and-shopify) and [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do a practice sale with your supervisor">
    Your supervisor will watch you ring up one practice sale. They will mark it as a test afterward so it does not count in the day's sales.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the right item

Every product and service in Shopify already belongs to a business unit and a category. This is how the daily sales report knows where each sale goes. You do not choose the category yourself. You only need to pick the correct item.

<Warning>
  Do not use **Custom sale** to type in an item by hand. A custom sale has no category, so it cannot be counted in the daily sales report. If you cannot find an item, ask your supervisor.
</Warning>

## When something goes wrong

- Stop and call your supervisor. Do not guess.
- Do not delete, redo, or refund a sale on your own.
- If the tablet or printer stops working, keep serving customers in Loyverse and tell your supervisor right away.
