For now, SixthGear uses two systems at the counter. Loyverse is the system you already know. Shopify POS is the new one. During the trial period, you record every sale in both.

<Info>
  This page only applies during the trial period. When management removes Loyverse, you will use Shopify POS alone and this page will be retired.
</Info>

## Why every sale goes in twice

- **Loyverse is still the official record** of the day's sales and money until management decides to switch.
- **Shopify keeps the stock correct for the website.** The website and Shopify POS share the same stock. If a helmet is sold only in Loyverse, the website still shows it as available and could sell it to an online customer.
- **Comparing both systems every day** proves that Shopify gives the same totals. When they match for enough days and everyone is comfortable with Shopify, Loyverse will be removed.

## Record a sale in both systems

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ring up the sale in Loyverse">
    Do it the same way you do today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ring up the same sale in Shopify POS right away">
    Do not wait until later or the end of the day. Use the same items, quantities, and discount you used in Loyverse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the same payment method in Shopify">
    If the customer paid by QR PH in Loyverse, choose **QR PH** in Shopify too. For split payments, use the same amounts in both. See [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the customer the Loyverse receipt">
    During the trial, the Loyverse receipt is the one the customer keeps. If the Shopify printer also prints a receipt, keep it with the day's papers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What must match

| Detail | Must be the same in both systems |
| --- | --- |
| Items and quantity | Yes |
| Price | Yes |
| Discount | Yes |
| Payment method | Yes. Cash, QR PH, card terminal, or Care of Boss |
| Split payment amounts | Yes |

## Refunds and returns

A refund must also be done in both systems. Never do a refund on your own. Call your supervisor, who will approve it and do it in both Loyverse and Shopify.

## At the end of the day

Your supervisor compares the day's total in Loyverse with the daily sales report from Shopify. If the two totals are different, they will look for the sale that does not match. They may ask you about a sale you handled, so keep the day's receipts together.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What goes wrong | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The sale was put in Loyverse but not in Shopify | The website shows the wrong stock, and the totals do not match | Tell your supervisor as soon as you notice |
| A different payment method was chosen in each system | The cash count and the report do not match | Tell your supervisor before the end of the day |
| **Custom sale** was used in Shopify | The sale has no category, so it is missing from the report | Tell your supervisor. Always pick the real item |
| The sale was put in Shopify but not in Loyverse | The official Loyverse total is too low | Tell your supervisor as soon as you notice |
