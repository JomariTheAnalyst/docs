Ringing up a sale means adding the items the customer is buying to the cart. This page covers the basics that work the same for every business unit. For services, carwash, hauling, and the café, also read their own pages.

## Add items to the cart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Tap the search bar and type part of the item's name, for example "shoei" or "latte." You can also scan the item's barcode with the scan button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right version">
    Many items come in versions, such as size, color, hot or iced. Tap the correct one. The price on the screen should match the price tag or menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the cart">
    The item appears in the cart with its price. Add the next item the same way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or remove an item

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Sell more than one of the same item | Tap the item in the cart and change the quantity |
| Remove an item | Tap the item in the cart and remove it |
| Change the size or version | Remove the item and add the correct version |

## Before you tap Checkout

- Read the cart back to the customer: the items, the quantities, and the total.
- If there is a discount, apply it now. See [Discounts](/at-the-counter/discounts).
- Then continue to [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment).

<Warning>
  Do not use **Custom sale** to type in an item by hand. A custom sale has no category, so the daily sales report cannot count it. If you cannot find an item, ask your supervisor.
</Warning>

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The item does not come up in search | Try a shorter word, or search by brand. If it still does not appear, ask your supervisor |
| The barcode does not scan | Search for the item by name instead |
| The price on the screen is different from the tag | Do not sell it at either price yet. Ask your supervisor which price is correct |
| The item shows as out of stock but it is on the shelf | If Shopify lets you continue, sell it and tell your supervisor so the stock count can be fixed. If it does not let you continue, call your supervisor |
