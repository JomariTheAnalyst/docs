## How payments work at SixthGear

Shopify never touches the money. Cash goes in the drawer. QR PH goes straight to the store's bank or e-wallet. Cards are charged on the bank's card terminal.

Shopify only records how each sale was paid. The daily sales report uses this to sort every sale into Cash and Non-Cash. At closing, your supervisor uses it to check the drawer, the card slips, and the QR payments.

## The Payment options screen

After you tap **Checkout**, you see a screen called **Payment options**. It has six buttons.

| Button | What it means | Where the money goes | In the report |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash** | The customer pays with bills and coins | The cash drawer | Cash |
| **Card Terminal** | The customer pays by credit or debit card on the bank's card terminal | The store's bank account, through the bank's terminal | Non-Cash |
| **QR PH** | The customer scans the store's QR code with GCash, Maya, or their bank app | The store's bank or e-wallet account | Non-Cash |
| **Care of Boss** | The owner is treating a guest and has approved it | No money is received | Not counted as income |
| **Split payment** | Not a payment method. It lets one sale use two or more methods | Each part goes where its own method sends it | Each part is counted under its own method |
| **Mark unpaid** | Finishes the sale with no money received. Staff never use it | No money is received. The customer still owes it | Shows as unpaid |

## Cash

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Checkout">
    Check that the items and total are correct first. You see **Payment options**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Payment options, tap Cash">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount the customer gave you">
    Tap one of the suggested amounts or type the exact amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the change shown on the screen">
    The screen shows how much change to give. Put the cash in the drawer before you hand over the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## QR PH

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show the customer the store's QR PH code">
    Tell them the exact amount to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that the money arrived">
    Look at the store's payment app or phone and find the payment with the correct amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Checkout">
    Only do this after you have seen the payment arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Payment options, tap QR PH">
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never accept a screenshot from the customer's phone as proof of payment. Screenshots can be old or edited. Only trust the payment you see arrive in the store's own app.
</Warning>

## Card terminal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the amount on the bank's card terminal">
    Let the customer tap or insert their card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the approved slip">
    If the terminal says the card was declined, do not continue. Ask the customer for another way to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Checkout">
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Payment options, tap Card Terminal">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the store's copy of the card slip">
    Put it with the day's papers for the end-of-day check.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Shopify does not charge the card. The bank's terminal charges it. **Card Terminal** in Shopify only records that the customer paid by card.
</Info>

## Care of Boss

Use this only when the owner treats a guest, for example coffee during a meeting, and has approved it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ring up the items as usual">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note to the sale">
    In the cart, add a note with who approved it and the reason, for example "Approved by the owner, meeting guests."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Checkout">
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Payment options, tap Care of Boss">
  </Step>
</Steps>

The items still come out of stock. This is correct, because they were used. The sale is not counted as income.

<Warning>
  Never use **Care of Boss** without the owner's approval. It removes the sale from the day's sales total.
</Warning>

## Split payment

Use a split payment when the customer pays with two or more methods, for example part cash and part card.

**Example:** the total is ₱37,400. The customer pays ₱20,000 in cash and the rest by card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Checkout">
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Payment options, tap Split payment">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter 20,000">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Cash">
    The screen shows ₱17,400 still unpaid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charge ₱17,400 on the bank's card terminal">
    Wait for the approved slip before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Card Terminal">
    The sale is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the daily sales report, this sale shows ₱20,000 as Cash and ₱17,400 as Non-Cash.

## Mark unpaid

**Mark unpaid** finishes a sale with no money received. The items leave stock, and the customer still owes the money.

<Warning>
  Never use **Mark unpaid**. If a customer cannot pay, call your supervisor.
</Warning>

## After payment

The receipt prints on the Epson printer. During the trial period, the customer keeps the Loyverse receipt. See [Using Loyverse and Shopify together](/at-the-counter/loyverse-and-shopify).

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The receipt did not print | Check that the printer is on, has paper, and its light is not blinking. Tell your supervisor if it still does not print |
| You chose the wrong payment method | Do not refund or redo the sale yourself. Tell your supervisor right away |
| The QR PH payment has not arrived | Wait and check again. Do not complete the sale until you see the payment |
| The card terminal is not working | Ask the customer to pay by cash or QR PH, and tell your supervisor |
| The customer cannot pay | Do not tap **Mark unpaid**. Call your supervisor |
