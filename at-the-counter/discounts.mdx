A discount lowers the price the customer pays. Every discount needs your supervisor's approval, and during the trial period it must be the same in Loyverse and Shopify.

## Discounts we give

| Discount | Who approves it |
| --- | --- |
| 10% | Your supervisor |
| 15% | Your supervisor |
| 20% | Your supervisor |
| Executive discount | The owner only. The owner sets the amount |

## Senior Citizen and PWD discounts

Call your supervisor for every Senior Citizen or PWD customer. Your supervisor will apply the discount and check the customer's ID.

## Apply a discount

You can give a discount on one item or on the whole sale.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get approval">
    Ask your supervisor before you apply any discount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where the discount goes">
    For one item, tap that item in the cart. For the whole sale, use the discount option for the whole cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the discount">
    Choose percentage, enter the number, for example **10**, and add the reason, for example "Supervisor approved 10%."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the new total">
    The cart shows the discount and the new total. Read the new total to the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always apply the discount in Shopify. Do not lower the price another way, such as using **Custom sale** or selling fewer items. The daily sales report needs to see the discount to count it correctly.
</Warning>

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You applied the wrong discount | Remove it from the cart and apply the correct one before checkout |
| You already finished the sale with the wrong discount | Tell your supervisor. Do not refund or redo it yourself |
| The discount option is not available to you | Your PIN may not have permission. Ask your supervisor to apply it |
