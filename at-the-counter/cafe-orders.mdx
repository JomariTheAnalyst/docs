Café items are sold at the counter only. Each drink and food item already belongs to the Sixth Gear Café business unit and the right category, such as Coffee Sales or Food and Snacks.

## The choices you will see

| Drink | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Most drinks | **Hot 12oz**, **Hot 16oz**, or **Cold 16oz**. Only the sizes on the menu for that drink appear |
| Espresso drinks | **Single** or **Double** |
| Fruit frappes | **Cream** or **Yogurt** |
| One-size drinks | No choices. The size is in the name, for example **Coffee Frappe (16oz)** |

## Take a café order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the drink or food">
    For example, search "latte" or "cheesecake."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose exactly what the customer ordered">
    Pick the size, or Single or Double, or Cream or Yogurt. One-size drinks go straight into the cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add any extras as their own item">
    **Extra Shot Espresso** and **Oat Milk** are separate items. Add them to the cart after the drink.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the order back to the customer">
    Confirm each drink and its choice before you tap **Checkout**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the owner treats a guest

If the owner treats a guest, for example coffee during a meeting, ring up the items as usual and pay with **Care of Boss**. You need the owner's approval every time. The owner pays the store back later. See [Care of Boss](/at-the-counter/take-payment#care-of-boss).

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The customer changes their order after you added it | Remove the wrong item from the cart and add the right one |
| A drink or food item is not in the list | Ask your supervisor. Do not use **Custom sale** |
| The customer wants a discount | See [Discounts](/at-the-counter/discounts) |
