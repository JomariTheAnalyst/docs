This is one normal day, start to finish. Read it once before your first shift. Each part links to the full page if you need more.

POS means point of sale. It is the app on the store tablet where you record what the customer bought and how they paid. During the trial, you also record every sale in Loyverse, the system you already know.

## 1. Log in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the tablet and the printer">
    The printer light should be steady, not blinking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Shopify POS">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your own PIN">
    Every sale is saved under your PIN. Never use someone else's.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Open the register

<Steps>
  <Step title="Count the cash in the drawer">
    This is the starting cash your supervisor gave you for change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the cash session and enter that amount">
  </Step>
</Steps>

More detail: [Open and close the register](/at-the-counter/open-and-close-register).

## 3. Ring up a sale

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Search by name or scan the barcode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the right version">
    For example, the right size, or Hot or Iced for a café drink.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the cart">
    The items, quantities, and total must match what the customer is buying.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never use **Custom sale**. If you cannot find an item, ask your supervisor.
</Warning>

More detail: [Ring up a sale](/at-the-counter/ring-up-a-sale). Services, carwash, hauling, and the café have their own pages in the same section.

## 4. Take payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Checkout">
    You see **Payment options**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap how the customer paid">
    **Cash**, **Card Terminal**, or **QR PH**. For QR PH, tap it only after the money shows in the store's QR PH app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="For cash, give the change shown on the screen">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the same sale in Loyverse">
    Same items, same payment method. The customer keeps the Loyverse receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two buttons you will not use on your first day: **Care of Boss** needs the owner's approval, and **Mark unpaid** is never used by staff.

More detail: [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment) and [Using Loyverse and Shopify together](/at-the-counter/loyverse-and-shopify).

## If something goes wrong

Call your supervisor. If the internet, printer, or tablet stops working, see [When things go wrong](/operations/when-things-go-wrong). Keep the tablet on and logged in.
