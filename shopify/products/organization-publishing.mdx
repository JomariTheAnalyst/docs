Use this guide to organize a product and control whether it appears on the SixthGear storefront and other approved sales channels.

<Info>
  Open **Shopify Admin → Products**, select the product, then review its **Product organization**, **Status**, and **Publishing** settings.
</Info>

## Product organization

| Field | How SixthGear uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Helps classify the product using Shopify's product categories |
| Product type | Groups similar products for internal management |
| Vendor | Records the approved brand or supplier name |
| Collections | Places the product in customer-facing or internal groups |
| Tags | Supports filters, search, automatic collections, and internal organization |

## Organize and publish the product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the product is complete">
    Check the title, description, images, variants, price, SKU, barcode, and inventory before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the category and type">
    Choose the closest approved category and use the existing SixthGear product-type wording where possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the vendor or brand">
    Use the approved brand name consistently. Avoid creating a second spelling or version of an existing vendor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add approved tags">
    Use existing tags with the same spelling and format. Tags may affect filters and automatic collections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add collections">
    Add the product to the correct manual collections or confirm that it meets the rules of the intended automatic collections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the product status">
    Use **Draft** while work is incomplete, **Active** when approved for sale, and **Archived** only when the product should no longer be used as an active record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sales channels">
    Select the approved channels in the publishing area. Confirm that the SixthGear headless storefront channel is included when the product must appear on the live website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the live result">
    Check the product page, collection pages, filters, search, and mobile display on the live SixthGear storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Product status guide

| Status | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | The product is incomplete, waiting for information, or not yet approved |
| Active | The product is complete and approved for its selected sales channels |
| Archived | The product is no longer active but its record should be retained |

<Warning>
  Active status alone does not guarantee that a product appears on the live website. The correct sales channel, inventory, collection rules, and storefront data must also be available.
</Warning>

## Before changing tags or collections

- Check whether an existing tag already has the same meaning.
- Check whether an automatic collection uses the tag.
- Check whether filters depend on the tag spelling.
- Do not rename `brand-*` collection handles without an approved storefront change.
- Do not remove a product from a collection until its customer-facing effect is understood.

## Final check

- [ ] Category and product type are correct
- [ ] Vendor or brand spelling is consistent
- [ ] Tags use approved wording
- [ ] Collections are correct
- [ ] Product status is intended
- [ ] Required sales channels are selected
- [ ] Product appears in the correct live pages and filters

<Check>
  The task is complete when the product is organized consistently and appears only in the approved sales channels, collections, and storefront areas.
</Check>
