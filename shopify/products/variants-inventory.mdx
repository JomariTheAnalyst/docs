Use this guide when a product has different sizes, colors, finishes, or other options, or when staff need to update its price or stock.

<Info>
  Go to **Shopify Admin → Products**, open the product, then review the **Variants** and **Inventory** areas.
</Info>

## What each variant needs

Each sellable variant should have the correct:

- Option values, such as size and color
- Price and compare-at price, when used
- SKU
- Barcode, when available
- Inventory quantity
- Inventory location
- Variant image, when the appearance is different
- Weight or shipping information, when required

## Add or update variants

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the approved options">
    List the actual sizes, colors, styles, or finishes before creating combinations. Avoid slightly different spellings for the same option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the product variants">
    In the product record, open the option or variant controls and confirm the current combinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or edit option values">
    Enter clear option names and values. Review the combinations Shopify creates before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete every variant record">
    Check the price, SKU, barcode, image, weight, and stock for every variant. Do not leave copied information from another option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the correct inventory location">
    Confirm which SixthGear location holds the stock. Enter only an approved quantity supported by the physical count or stock record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test the product selector">
    Open the live product page and select every option. Confirm that the image, price, availability, and add-to-cart result match the selected variant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing rules

- Confirm the approved selling price before editing.
- Check every variant when prices differ by size, model, or option.
- Use a compare-at price only when it is valid and approved.
- Do not use the compare-at field to create a false discount.
- Do not change a live price only to correct an unrelated display problem.

## Inventory adjustment rules

| Situation | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| New stock received | Confirm the item, variant, quantity, and location before adding stock |
| Physical count is lower | Check recent sales, returns, transfers, and earlier adjustments before reducing stock |
| Physical count is higher | Confirm that receiving or a return was not missed before increasing stock |
| Damaged or missing item | Record the approved reason and responsible person |
| Customer return | Restore stock only when the item can be sold again and only once |
| Shopify and Loyverse differ | Follow the approved reconciliation process; do not copy a number only to make them match |

<Warning>
  Physical-store inventory ownership between Shopify and Loyverse is still subject to the approved POS process. Review [Commerce Data Ownership](/operations/commerce-data-ownership) before correcting a cross-system difference.
</Warning>

## Final check

- [ ] Option names and values are consistent
- [ ] All variant combinations are valid
- [ ] Every variant has the correct price
- [ ] SKUs and barcodes are correct and not duplicated
- [ ] Inventory is assigned to the correct location
- [ ] Variant images are correct
- [ ] Live option selection was tested
- [ ] Any manual stock adjustment has a recorded reason

<Check>
  The task is complete when customers can select the correct option and Shopify shows the approved price, identifier, image, and available stock for that exact variant.
</Check>
