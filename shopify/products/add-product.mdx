Use this page to add a product that is sold both on the website and at the counter, such as a helmet, riding gear, a part, or an accessory. For services, carwash, hauling, and café items, use [Add a counter-only item](/products-and-stock/add-counter-item) instead.

## Before you start

Have this information ready:

- Product name, brand, and a short description
- Clear product photos
- Price for each size or color, and the compare-at price if the item is on sale
- SKU and barcode for each size or color
- Stock count for each size or color
- The business unit and report category. See [How products work](/products-and-stock/overview)

## Add the product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add product">
    In Shopify admin, go to **Products** and click **Add product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the title and description">
    Use the full product name, for example "SHOEI GT-Air 3 Matte Black." Staff will search for this name on the counter tablet, so include the brand and model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the photos">
    Upload clear photos. The first photo is the main one shown on the website, so put the best one first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the price">
    Enter the selling price. Only fill in **Compare-at price** if the item is really on sale. Never leave the price at ₱0, because the website cannot show a ₱0 price properly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sizes or colors">
    If the item comes in sizes or colors, add them as options. Check the price, SKU, and barcode of every size or color. Do not copy the SKU or barcode of another product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the stock">
    Leave **Track quantity** on and enter how many of each size or color you have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the product organization">
    Choose the **Product type** and **Vendor**. If the list shows two spellings of the same name, ask your supervisor which one to use. Add the product to its brand collection. You do not need to add it to **All Products**: every product joins that collection by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the two labels">
    Scroll to the bottom of the page and fill in **Business unit** and **Report category**. For website products, the business unit is almost always `Retail`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on both sales channels">
    In **Sales channels**, turn on the website's sales channel and **Point of Sale**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status and save">
    Set the status to **Active** when everything is correct, then click **Save**. If some details are still missing, keep it as **Draft** and finish it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

1. Wait about 15 minutes, then search for the product on the website. Check the photo, price, and sizes.
2. On the counter tablet, search for the product. Check that the price is the same.
3. During the trial period, add the same product to Loyverse with the same price.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Shopify will not save the product | Check the **Business unit** and **Report category** spelling. They must match the list exactly |
| The product is not on the website | Check that the status is **Active** and the website's sales channel is on. Wait 15 minutes and check again |
| The product is not on the tablet | Check that **Point of Sale** is on in **Sales channels** |
| The brand shows twice in the website filters | The **Vendor** was spelled differently from the existing one. Fix the spelling |
