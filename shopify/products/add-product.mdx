Use this guide when adding a new product to the SixthGear Shopify store.

The product is complete when its information, images, variants, price, stock, organization, sales channels, and live storefront page have been checked.

<Info>
  Go to **Shopify Admin → Products → Add product**.
</Info>

## Before you begin

Prepare the approved product information before opening Shopify:

- Product name and brand
- Product description
- Product images
- Product category or type
- Price and compare-at price, when used
- SKU and barcode
- Available sizes, colors, or other options
- Starting stock for each variant
- Weight or shipping details
- Tags and collection membership
- Approved sales channels

<Warning>
  Shopify is the main record for product titles, descriptions, prices, variants, SKUs, barcodes, inventory, collections, and sales-channel availability. Do not create a second product price or stock value in SixthGear CMS.
</Warning>

## Add the product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct store">
    Sign in to Shopify and confirm that the SixthGear Moto store is open before making changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Add product">
    Go to **Products** and select **Add product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the title and description">
    Use the approved product name. Write a clear description that explains what the product is, its main features, fitment or use, and any important customer information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and arrange product images">
    Upload clear product images. Place the strongest main image first because Shopify uses the first media item as the featured image in several areas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the product category and organization">
    Add the correct product category, vendor or brand, product type, tags, and collections. Follow the existing SixthGear naming and tag rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the approved price">
    Add the selling price. Use a compare-at price only when it represents an approved original price or promotion. Do not guess the cost or selling price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add variants when needed">
    Add options such as size, color, style, or finish. Review every option combination before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete each SKU and barcode">
    Enter the correct SKU and barcode for every variant. Do not reuse another product's SKU or barcode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set inventory and location">
    Turn on inventory tracking when required, select the correct location, and enter the approved starting stock for each variant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add shipping information">
    Confirm whether the item is physical and add the correct weight or other required shipping details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sales channels">
    In the publishing area, select only the approved channels. The product must be available to the SixthGear headless storefront channel for it to appear on the live website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the search listing and handle">
    Check the search title, description, and URL handle. Do not change a handle after publication without checking links and redirects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as Draft until complete">
    Keep the product as **Draft** while required information is missing. Set it to **Active** only after the product is approved and ready for customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the live product page">
    Open the live SixthGear storefront and check the product on desktop and mobile. Confirm the title, images, price, variants, stock message, collections, and purchase flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important SixthGear rules

- Do not copy another product and leave its SKU, barcode, images, tags, collections, or metafields unchanged.
- Every variant must have the correct price, SKU, barcode, and stock.
- Do not publish an incomplete product only to test whether access works.
- Do not assume that the Shopify theme preview is the live website. Check the live Next.js storefront.
- Do not change payment, billing, user, app, or domain settings while adding a product.
- Record the reason for any manual inventory adjustment.

## Final check

- [ ] Product title is correct
- [ ] Description is complete and customer-friendly
- [ ] Main image and supporting images are correct
- [ ] Price is approved
- [ ] Compare-at price is valid, when used
- [ ] Every variant has the correct SKU and barcode
- [ ] Inventory is assigned to the correct location
- [ ] Tags and collections are correct
- [ ] Required sales channels are selected
- [ ] Product status is correct
- [ ] Live product page works on desktop and mobile

<Check>
  The task is complete when the product is accurate in Shopify and the live SixthGear storefront shows the expected product, price, options, images, and availability.
</Check>
