Use this guide when changing an existing SixthGear product in Shopify.

<Info>
  Go to **Shopify Admin → Products**, then open the product you need to update.
</Info>

## Before editing

- Confirm the requested change and who approved it.
- Open the correct product and variant.
- Review the current title, price, stock, images, tags, collections, and sales channels.
- Check whether the product already has customer orders or active links.

<Warning>
  Do not edit another product because it looks similar. Confirm the title, SKU, barcode, and product URL before saving.
</Warning>

## Edit the product safely

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct product">
    In Shopify Admin, go to **Products**, search by title, SKU, or barcode, and open the correct record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change only the approved fields">
    Update the requested title, description, image, price, variant, stock, tag, collection, or sales-channel setting. Avoid unrelated changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review variants before saving">
    When editing size, color, price, SKU, barcode, or stock, check every affected variant. Do not assume one change applies correctly to all variants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect the product URL">
    Do not change the URL handle unless the new handle is approved and links or redirects have been checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and review Shopify">
    Save the product and confirm the updated values in Shopify before leaving the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the live storefront">
    Open the product on the live SixthGear website. Check desktop and mobile, including title, price, images, variants, stock, collection links, and add-to-cart behavior.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changes that need extra care

| Change | Main risk | Required check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product title | Confusing customers or staff | Confirm the approved name and live page |
| Price | Incorrect customer charge | Confirm approval and every variant price |
| SKU or barcode | Inventory and scanning errors | Confirm the identifier is unique and correct |
| Variant option | Broken combinations or wrong stock | Check all affected variants |
| Inventory | Overselling or missing stock | Confirm location, reason, and actual count |
| Product status | Product disappears or publishes early | Confirm Draft, Active, or Archived is intended |
| Sales channels | Product missing from the website | Confirm the correct storefront channel |
| URL handle | Broken links | Confirm redirects and live links |

## Do not do these

- Do not overwrite another variant's SKU or barcode.
- Do not reduce stock only to make two systems appear equal.
- Do not remove a product from a channel without checking where it is sold.
- Do not archive a product only because it is temporarily out of stock.
- Do not delete product media before confirming it is no longer used.

## Final check

- [ ] Correct product was edited
- [ ] Only approved fields changed
- [ ] Variant details remain correct
- [ ] Price and inventory were checked
- [ ] Product status and sales channels are correct
- [ ] Live page works on desktop and mobile
- [ ] Any problem or follow-up is recorded

<Check>
  The task is complete when Shopify and the live storefront show the approved change without affecting unrelated product information.
</Check>
