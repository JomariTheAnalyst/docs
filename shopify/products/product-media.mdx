Use this guide when adding or replacing product images and videos in Shopify.

<Info>
  Go to **Shopify Admin → Products**, open the product, then use the **Media** section.
</Info>

## Prepare the files

Use clear, approved media that shows the actual product. Before uploading, check:

- The product and variant are correct
- The image is sharp and not stretched
- The background and crop are suitable
- The file does not contain private information or an unapproved watermark
- Repeated images are removed
- The main image clearly represents the product

## Add and arrange media

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct product">
    Search by product title, SKU, or barcode and confirm the record before uploading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the media">
    Add the approved image or video in the product's Media section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange the order">
    Put the strongest product image first. Arrange the remaining media in a useful order, such as front, side, rear, detail, packaging, and fitment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect variant images">
    When colors or styles look different, assign the correct media to the matching variant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add alternative text">
    Describe what the image shows in a short, useful sentence. Do not fill it with repeated keywords.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Save the product, then check the image order, crop, loading, zoom, variant image, and mobile display on the live storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Image order guide

1. Main product view
2. Second useful angle
3. Opposite side or rear view
4. Important feature or material detail
5. Variant or color view
6. Fitment, sizing, or included items when relevant

<Warning>
  Do not remove the current main image until the replacement has been uploaded and checked. Removing media can affect product cards, search results, collection pages, and shared links.
</Warning>

## Alternative text example

Good:

> Black full-face motorcycle helmet shown from the left side.

Avoid:

> Helmet motorcycle helmet best helmet shop helmet Philippines.

## Final check

- [ ] Correct product was opened
- [ ] Main image is first
- [ ] Images are clear and correctly cropped
- [ ] Duplicate or incorrect media is removed
- [ ] Variant images match the correct options
- [ ] Alternative text is useful
- [ ] Live desktop and mobile display were checked

<Check>
  The task is complete when the product media clearly represents the item and displays correctly across the live product and collection pages.
</Check>
