## Request and invite a named user

Use an individual company email when a separate Shopify user can be created.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the required work">
    Record the person's business responsibility and the Shopify tasks they need to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the minimum required role">
    Choose an existing role or approved custom role that provides only the permissions needed for the recorded work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Shopify invitation">
    An authorized Shopify user administrator opens **Settings → Users**, enters the approved company email, assigns the approved role or roles, and sends the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    The recipient opens the Shopify invitation, signs in with their own Shopify account, and confirms that the invitation belongs to the SixthGear store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure MFA">
    The recipient enables and controls MFA for their own named account through an approved method. Recovery codes must be kept in approved secure storage and never copied into Mintlify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and record access">
    Confirm the user status, assigned roles, required Shopify areas, and MFA setup. Record the completed handoff below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## MFA responsibilities

| Access type | Required control |
| --- | --- |
| Named Shopify user | The user completes MFA through their own registered method and securely stores their own recovery information. |
| Company-controlled account | The assigned MFA custodian verifies the person, store, purpose, and request time before approving the sign-in. |
| Backup recovery | A company-approved backup recovery route must be documented outside Mintlify before the access handoff is considered complete. |
| Unexpected MFA request | Deny or ignore the request, notify the responsible administrator, and review account activity. |

<Warning>
  Do not send one-time codes, recovery codes, authenticator screenshots, or approval links through chat, ordinary email, or Mintlify.
</Warning>

## Password and access custody

| Responsibility | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Password custodian | Maintains the approved password-manager record for the company-controlled account. This does not automatically authorize sharing or retrieving the password. |
| Password access approver | Authorizes emergency or owner-level password retrieval according to the approved company process. |
| MFA custodian | Verifies and approves MFA requests for the company-controlled account. The custodian does not act as the MFA provider for named users. |
| Shopify user administrator | Sends invitations, assigns approved roles, reviews user status, and removes access when instructed. |
| Handler in supplied inventory | Identifies the person associated with the supplied record. It does not by itself authorize password retrieval, role changes, or Shopify administration. |

## Choose the minimum required role

| Business responsibility | Access that may be required | Keep restricted unless separately approved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Management oversight | Dashboard, analytics, reports, and order visibility | Users, apps, domains, billing, and payment settings |
| Product management | Products, collections, inventory, and files | Billing, users, and payment settings |
| Customer service | Orders, customers, and approved refund actions | Customer exports, users, apps, and billing |
| Finance | Approved billing, payout, payment, and financial-report areas | Products, apps, domains, and user administration when not required |
| Shopify administration | User invitations, role assignment, store settings, and approved security work | Ownership-level actions outside the approved task |

<Note>
  A user can receive more than one role. Review the combined permissions before approval because permissions from assigned roles accumulate.
</Note>

## Sensitive permissions

The following permissions require explicit approval because they can affect company ownership, money, customer information, integrations, or store availability:

- Users and role management
- Billing, plans, payouts, and finance
- Payment-provider and checkout settings
- App installation and removal
- Domains and primary-store settings
- Customer-data exports
- Discounts and gift cards
- Ownership transfer, store pause, or deactivation

<Warning>
  Do not grant a broad role only to make setup faster. Record the business reason and approving authority before assigning sensitive permissions.
</Warning>

## Remove or suspend access

Use this process when a person changes role, leaves the company, no longer needs Shopify, or may have unauthorized access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the removal authority">
    Obtain instruction from management or the authorized Shopify user administrator before changing access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review open responsibilities">
    Transfer unfinished orders, customer cases, product work, reports, approvals, and other assigned responsibilities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suspend or remove the Shopify user">
    Open **Settings → Users** and suspend or remove the named user according to the approved instruction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove related access">
    Remove access to approved password-manager records, connected apps, shared devices, documentation, and other systems associated with the person's Shopify responsibilities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review sensitive activity">
    Check recent user activity, role changes, app changes, exports, billing, payment settings, and other sensitive actions when risk or exposure is suspected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm completion">
    Verify that the person can no longer open the SixthGear Shopify store and record the removal date, approver, and any follow-up action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Access removal is complete when the user is no longer active, business responsibilities have been transferred, related access has been removed, and the action is recorded.
</Check>
