## Sign in and verify access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Shopify Admin">
    Open the approved Shopify sign-in link and use the company-controlled account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete MFA">
    Coordinate with Stephanie Grace Lelina through the approved company process. Do not place a one-time code in chat, email documentation, or Mintlify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the store">
    Verify that the store is SixthGear Moto before changing products, payments, users, billing, or settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the management role">
    Open **Settings → Users** and confirm that your boss has the approved permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the required areas">
    Confirm access to **Products**, **Orders**, **Customers**, **Settings**, and **Users** according to the assigned role.
  </Step>
</Steps>
