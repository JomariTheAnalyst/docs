## Receive and accept an invitation

{/* Component: Steps */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="The administrator sends the invitation">
    An authorized Shopify administrator opens **Settings → Users**, selects **Add users**, enters the recipient's email address, assigns the approved role, and sends the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Shopify invitation email">
    Open the invitation sent to the approved email address. Check **Spam**, **Junk**, and **Promotions** if it does not arrive in the main inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation within seven days">
    Select **Accept invite**. Shopify invitations expire after seven days. Ask the administrator to resend the invitation when the link has expired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or create a Shopify account">
    Sign in using the exact email address that received the invitation. Create a Shopify account when the email is not yet connected to one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete account security">
    Complete any email verification or two-step authentication requested by Shopify. Use the recipient's own security method for a named account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the store opens">
    The SixthgearMoto Shopify Admin should open after the invitation is accepted. Do not make changes until the store and permissions have been checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* Component: Note */}

<Note>
  Do not forward a Shopify invitation. The invited email address becomes the identity connected to the assigned store role.
</Note>

## Confirm your permissions

Shopify access is controlled by assigned roles and permissions. The store owner has ownership-level authority. Other users receive one or more roles created or assigned by an authorized administrator.

{/* Component: CardGroup */}

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Store owner" icon="crown">
    Controls store ownership and the highest-level account responsibilities. Ownership is held by one store owner at a time.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Administrator or staff user" icon="user-cog">
    Receives only the roles and permissions assigned for products, orders, customers, settings, apps, or other approved work.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

{/* Component: Steps */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the left navigation">
    Confirm that the sections required for the approved task are visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the required section without changing data">
    Open **Products**, **Collections**, **Orders**, or another approved section and confirm that the expected controls are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request missing access">
    Ask the store owner or a user administrator to review the assigned role when an approved section or action is unavailable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation not received | Wrong email, spam filtering, or invitation not sent | Check Spam, Junk, and Promotions. Ask the administrator to confirm the exact email and pending invitation. |
| Invitation expired | More than seven days passed | Ask an authorized administrator to resend the invitation from **Settings → Users**. |
| Add users is unavailable | The plan does not support additional admin users or the current user lacks permission | Confirm the current Shopify plan and the signed-in user's role. Use the controlled owner-account process only when approved. |
| A section is missing | The assigned role does not include it | Ask the store owner or user administrator to review the role. |
| Wrong store opened | The account is connected to multiple stores | Use the store-specific SixthgearMoto Admin URL and the store switcher. |
| Password forgotten | The Shopify account password is unavailable | Use Shopify's account recovery flow for the affected named account. |
| Two-step authentication unavailable | The authenticator, phone, passkey, or recovery code cannot be used | Use the account's approved recovery method. For the business owner account, contact the documented MFA and recovery custodians. |
