Use this guide when a customer order appears in Shopify and staff need to prepare, release, ship, or complete it.

<Info>
  Go to **Shopify Admin → Orders**, then open the correct order.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Do not release, ship, or mark an order fulfilled until the payment status and the correct items have been checked. For normal prepaid orders, wait until the payment status is **Paid** unless management has approved a different written process.
</Warning>

## Before processing the order

Check the following before touching the products:

- Correct SixthGear Shopify store is open
- Correct order number and customer
- Payment status is clear
- Order is not cancelled
- Shopify does not show an unresolved risk warning
- Product, variant, quantity, and price are correct
- Delivery, shipping, or pickup method is clear
- Customer address and contact details are complete when needed
- Customer notes or delivery instructions have been reviewed
- Required stock is available at the assigned location

## Understand the main statuses

### Payment status

| Status | What it means | Staff action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paid** | Payment has been captured or the order was correctly marked paid | Continue with the order checks |
| **Pending** | The payment provider is still processing the payment | Wait and check the provider record; do not release the order |
| **Authorized** | Payment was approved but has not yet been captured | Ask the authorized person to follow the approved payment-capture process |
| **Unpaid** | Full payment has not been received | Do not fulfill or release the order |
| **Partially paid** | Only part of the total has been received | Stop and ask the responsible person to review the balance |
| **Refunded** | The full payment was returned | Do not process as a normal paid order |
| **Partially refunded** | Part of the payment was returned | Confirm which items and amount remain valid before continuing |

### Fulfillment status

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Unfulfilled** | The items have not yet been released, shipped, or completed |
| **In progress** | Staff have started preparing the order |
| **Partially fulfilled** | Only some items or quantities have been completed |
| **Fulfilled** | All required items have been released, shipped, picked up, or completed |
| **On hold** | Fulfillment must not continue until the hold is released |

<Note>
  **Paid** and **Fulfilled** are not the same. Paid means the money is confirmed. Fulfilled means the products were released, shipped, picked up, or completed.
</Note>

## Process a normal paid order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct order">
    In **Orders**, search by order number or customer name. Confirm the order number before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review payment and warnings">
    Confirm the payment status is **Paid** for a normal prepaid order. Review the order timeline and any risk, payment, cancellation, or hold messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the products">
    Confirm each product, variant, size, color, model, quantity, and price against the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the delivery method">
    Confirm whether the order is for shipping, local delivery, or pickup. Follow only the method selected on the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the correct items">
    Take the products from the assigned location. Match each item to the order and inspect it for damage, missing parts, or incorrect packaging.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pack and label the order">
    Pack the products safely. Use the approved SixthGear packing, receipt, and courier process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark only prepared items as fulfilled">
    In the **Unfulfilled** or **In progress** section, select **Mark as fulfilled**. Confirm the exact items and quantities before completing the action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tracking when shipping">
    Enter the correct tracking number and carrier when available. Confirm the tracking number belongs to this customer and package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the correct notification">
    Keep the customer notification selected when the approved process requires Shopify to send shipment details. Review the email address before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the final order status">
    Reopen the order and confirm the payment, fulfillment, items, quantities, tracking, timeline, and remaining work are correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Partial fulfillment

Use partial fulfillment only when some items are ready and the customer or approved process allows separate release or delivery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm which items are ready">
    Check the exact products and quantities that can be released now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the remaining plan">
    Record why the other items are delayed and who will follow up with the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fulfill only the ready quantities">
    Select **Mark as fulfilled**, then enter only the quantities included in this shipment or release.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the correct tracking details">
    Add tracking only for the package being sent now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the remaining items">
    Confirm the order shows **Partially fulfilled** and the unfinished items remain visible for follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not mark the whole order fulfilled when one or more items are still waiting for stock, customer approval, preparation, or delivery.
</Warning>

## Pickup orders

Use this section only when pickup in store is enabled and the customer selected pickup during checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check payment and pickup location">
    Confirm the payment status, selected pickup location, customer, products, and quantities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare and label the order">
    Pick, inspect, and safely hold the products under the correct order number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the order ready for pickup">
    After every pickup item is ready, use **Ready for pickup** and confirm the customer notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the person collecting">
    Ask for the order number and follow the approved SixthGear customer or authorized-receiver verification process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release the correct items">
    Match the products and quantities to the order before handing them over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the order picked up">
    After the customer receives the items, select **Mark as picked up** and confirm the order is fulfilled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not hand over an order based only on a customer name. Confirm the order number and follow the approved pickup verification process.
</Warning>

## Local delivery orders

Use this section only when Shopify local delivery is enabled for the order.

1. Review the delivery address, contact details, and customer instructions.
2. Pick and pack the correct items.
3. Use **Prepare delivery** and mark the order **Ready for delivery** when it is ready to leave.
4. Follow the approved SixthGear local delivery process.
5. After delivery is confirmed, select **Mark as delivered**.
6. Recheck that the order is fulfilled and no items remain open.

## Payment is pending or unclear

When the customer says they paid but Shopify does not show **Paid**:

1. Do not immediately ask the customer to pay again.
2. Do not mark the order paid without authority.
3. Check the order timeline and approved payment-provider record.
4. Record the amount, payment method, time, and available reference.
5. Ask the responsible payment or Shopify administrator to review it.
6. Keep the order unfulfilled until payment is confirmed or an approved exception is given.

A screenshot from the customer alone is not enough proof that SixthGear received the payment.

## Cancelled or stopped orders

- Do not prepare or release a cancelled order.
- Check whether payment was captured and whether a refund or further action is required.
- Confirm whether inventory was restored.
- Keep the order record and timeline intact.
- Do not delete or change information only to hide the cancellation.
- Escalate any order that was cancelled after packing, shipping, or customer pickup preparation began.

## Staff boundaries

Do not perform these actions without the required approval:

- Mark an unpaid order as paid
- Capture an authorized payment
- Cancel an order
- Issue a refund
- Change a paid order's price, items, discount, or shipping charge
- Change customer information without a valid reason
- Release an order with unclear payment
- Ignore a Shopify risk warning or fulfillment hold
- Fulfill items that were not actually prepared
- Change payment, checkout, shipping, app, user, or billing settings

## Final order check

- [ ] Correct order number and customer
- [ ] Payment status confirmed
- [ ] No unresolved cancellation, risk warning, or hold
- [ ] Correct products and variants
- [ ] Correct quantities
- [ ] Items inspected before release
- [ ] Delivery, shipping, or pickup method followed
- [ ] Tracking is correct when used
- [ ] Only prepared items were fulfilled
- [ ] Customer notification was handled correctly
- [ ] Remaining items or follow-up actions are recorded

<Check>
  The task is complete when the customer receives the correct items, Shopify shows the correct payment and fulfillment status, and no unfinished item or follow-up is hidden.
</Check>
