<Info>
  **Selected direction:** proceed with Maya for the written quotation and one-counter Shopify POS pilot. PayMongo, Xendit, and eGovPay remain on this page for comparison only, in case management changes the provider later.
</Info>

## Rate status and limits

**Public rates verified:** July 27, 2026

The tables below use each provider's current public pricing pages. They are not SixthGear's final contracted rates.

- A merchant quotation can differ because of payment volume, merchant category, risk review, taxes, settlement terms, and negotiated discounts.
- A percentage charge is calculated against the payment amount unless the provider states a fixed or minimum fee.
- PayMongo publishes its rates as exclusive of VAT.
- Xendit's calculator displays VAT as a separate charge.
- Maya's public pricing page does not clearly state whether every listed rack rate includes or excludes VAT. Confirm this in writing.
- eGovPay does not publish a retail merchant rate table suitable for this comparison.

<Warning>
  Do not copy these public rates into a budget or approval record as final charges. Attach the provider's written quotation and record its issue date, validity period, VAT treatment, settlement fees, refund fees, and chargeback fees.
</Warning>

## Shopify fee added to online gateway payments

When SixthGear uses a third-party payment provider for online Shopify checkout, Shopify can charge a separate third-party transaction fee in addition to the provider's processing fee.

| Shopify plan | Shopify third-party transaction fee |
| --- | --: |
| Basic | 2.00% |
| Grow | 1.00% |
| Advanced | 0.60% |
| Plus | 0.20%, subject to the current Plus contract |

Shopify states that this fee does not apply to Shopify POS orders or approved manual payment methods. A Maya Terminal transaction recorded through a Shopify POS custom payment type still incurs Maya's merchant charge, but it is not an online gateway transaction.

## Why Maya is the selected direction

Maya is not selected only because of its public percentage rates. The intended advantage is one commercial relationship for:

- Maya Checkout on the Shopify website
- Maya Terminal for card, wallet, and QR payments at the physical counter
- Merchant settlement and transaction reporting
- A controlled Shopify POS pilot using external custom payment types

The final approval still depends on Maya's written commercial proposal and the pilot reconciliation results.

## Maya Terminal workflow with Shopify POS

The Maya Terminal is treated as an external payment terminal. Shopify POS does not automatically receive the terminal's authorization, refund, settlement, or chargeback details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the order in Shopify POS">
    Add the products and confirm the final amount on the Shopify POS device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process the same amount in Maya Terminal">
    Enter the amount and let the customer pay through an approved card, QR, or wallet method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the Maya approval">
    Confirm the approved status, exact amount, reference number, and payment method. Do not rely on a customer screenshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the Shopify POS payment">
    Select the matching approved custom payment type, such as **Maya Terminal - Card** or **Maya Terminal - QR**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile at closing">
    Compare Shopify POS payment-type totals with Maya's transaction and settlement reports. Investigate every difference before closing the day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Written quotation required from Maya

Before management signs, request one document that confirms:

| Commercial item | Required written detail |
| --- | --- |
| Terminal | Exact model, quantity, ownership, rental or subscription basis, deposit, installation, and delivery |
| Monthly charges | Terminal subscription, platform fee, account fee, and minimum volume |
| Cards | Exact MDR for Visa, Mastercard, BancNet, JCB, American Express, domestic cards, and international cards |
| Wallets and QR | Exact rates for Maya, GCash, QR Ph, WeChat Pay, ShopeePay, and every enabled method |
| Online Checkout | Exact card, wallet, QR, bank, plugin, and gateway charges |
| Taxes | VAT inclusion or exclusion and withholding-tax treatment |
| Settlement | Schedule, cutoff, bank-transfer fee, settlement account, and holiday handling |
| Refunds and disputes | Void, refund, retrieval request, chargeback, and dispute fees |
| Contract | Validity period, lock-in, renewal, early termination, and pricing-review terms |
| Support | Support hours, replacement time, warranty, backup-terminal policy, and escalation contact |
| Integration | Shopify plugin support, terminal or ECR integration, credentials, testing, and production activation |

## Maya application preparation

Prepare current corporate records through Maya's approved onboarding channel. The final checklist must come from Maya.

- SEC registration and current corporate records
- Current business or mayor's permit
- BIR Certificate of Registration
- Authorized signatory and beneficial-owner records
- Board resolution or secretary's certificate when required
- Company bank and settlement documents
- Current IDs for required representatives
- Expected monthly payment volume and average transaction value
- Website, store, product, and operating information requested during due diligence

Never place Maya credentials, API keys, terminal credentials, MFA codes, or bank details in Mintlify.
