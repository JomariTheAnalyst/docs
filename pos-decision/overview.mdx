<Info>
  **Recommendation:** Keep Shopify as the online commerce platform, keep Loyverse active for now, and test Shopify POS Lite at one counter for two to four weeks.
</Info>

## Why Shopify should remain online

The live SixthGear storefront depends on Shopify for products, collections, variants, inventory, customers, carts, orders, and hosted checkout. Removing Shopify would require a replacement commerce backend and significant storefront migration work.

## Why Loyverse should not be removed immediately

Loyverse may still provide a more familiar physical-store workflow and may work better with existing iMin or lower-cost POS hardware. A controlled pilot will show whether Shopify's unified inventory and orders provide enough practical benefit to justify migration.

## Approval rule

Do not cancel Shopify, remove Loyverse, or purchase a full hardware rollout until the pilot confirms:

- Accurate inventory after sales and returns
- Reliable receipt printing
- Acceptable card-payment recording
- Clear end-of-day reconciliation
- Stable hardware performance
- Staff readiness
- An approved total cost
