Use this page to collect the evidence management needs before retiring either Shopify or Loyverse.

<Info>
  Before the pilot starts, review [Commerce Data Ownership](/operations/commerce-data-ownership). The pilot must clearly state which system records the real sale, how stock is compared, and how duplicate revenue or inventory changes will be prevented.
</Info>

## Pilot scope

| Item | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Pilot counter | Store location and counter number |
| Pilot period | Start date and end date |
| Shopify plan | Current plan and whether POS Lite or POS Pro is used |
| POS device | Exact tablet or iMin model and operating-system version |
| Receipt printer | Exact manufacturer model and interface variant |
| Scanner | Camera, USB scanner, or wireless scanner model |
| Cash drawer | Exact model and printer connection |
| Payment terminal | Provider, model, and payment-recording method |
| Pilot owner | Person responsible for daily checks |
| Management approver | Person authorized to approve migration or rejection |

## Required test cases

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete a normal cash sale">
    Confirm product lookup, stock deduction, receipt printing, drawer opening, and cash reporting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete an external card sale">
    Process the payment through the approved terminal, record it in Shopify POS, and verify reconciliation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell a product with variants">
    Confirm the correct size, color, SKU, price, and stock location are selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply an approved discount">
    Check authorization, receipt presentation, reporting, and the final paid amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process a return or exchange">
    Verify stock restoration, refund recording, replacement-item deduction, and receipt history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test a temporary internet problem">
    Record what continues to work, what stops, and what must be reconciled after connectivity returns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete end-of-day reconciliation">
    Compare cash, terminal totals, Shopify POS records, Loyverse records during the parallel period, and physical variances.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Final decision options

### Approve full Shopify POS migration

Choose this only when the pilot proves that Shopify provides reliable store operations, acceptable payment handling, accurate inventory, and a justified total cost.

Required follow-up:

- Approve hardware quantities and budget
- Configure staff roles and locations
- Migrate open operational records
- Train all users
- Set a Loyverse cutoff date
- Export and archive Loyverse records
- Run final stock reconciliation
- Keep a rollback plan for the first production week

### Keep Shopify online and retain Loyverse POS

Choose this when Loyverse remains better for physical-store operations or current hardware, while Shopify continues as the ecommerce engine.

Required follow-up:

- Define the system of record for product fields and inventory
- Approve a synchronization tool or manual process
- Assign owners for sync failures and stock differences
- Document returns, transfers, and end-of-day reconciliation
- Review integration cost and reliability regularly

### Stop the Shopify POS pilot

Choose this when the pilot does not meet operational, hardware, payment, or cost requirements.

Stopping the pilot does not mean removing Shopify from the online store.
