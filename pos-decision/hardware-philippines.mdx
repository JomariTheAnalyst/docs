## All-in-one POS availability

Shopify does not currently list a supported all-in-one POS computer for Philippine merchants. The supported setup is a compatible iPad, iPhone, or Android device with separately supported printers, scanners, and cash drawers.

<Note>
  Shopify Retail Support confirmed that unsupported all-in-one devices and printers cannot be manually configured or guaranteed to work. The existing iMin device is therefore not approved for Shopify POS.
</Note>

## Cash drawer requirements

The cash drawer must be compatible with the selected receipt printer. Confirm:

- Connector type
- Drawer-kick voltage
- Pinout
- Printer support
- Key and manual-open function
- Local warranty
- Physical dimensions

Do not assume every generic RJ11 or RJ12 drawer will open correctly.
