Do these checks after the staff have [closed the register](/at-the-counter/open-and-close-register). They catch mistakes while the day is still fresh. During the trial period, Loyverse is the official record.

## The checks

| Check | Compare | Should match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash | The cash counted in the drawer | The expected cash in Shopify |
| Card | The approved card slips from the bank's card terminal | The **Card Terminal** total in Shopify |
| QR PH | The payments received in the store's QR PH app | The **QR PH** total in Shopify |
| Loyverse | The Loyverse total for the day | The Shopify total for the day |
| Care of Boss | The list of **Care of Boss** sales today | Each one has a note with the owner's approval |
| Custom sale | Any sale made with **Custom sale** | There should be none |

## Care of Boss sales for the owner

Care of Boss is a sale at full price. The owner pays the store back later. At closing, list every Care of Boss sale of the day with its order number, the amount, and the note. Give the list to the owner so they can reimburse the store.

When the owner pays back in cash, staff record it in the drawer. See [When the owner pays back a Care of Boss sale](/at-the-counter/open-and-close-register#when-the-owner-pays-back-a-care-of-boss-sale).

## Custom sale

Staff must never use **Custom sale**. It has no category, so the daily sales report cannot place it. If you find one, ask the staff member what was sold. Then tell the owner so the sale can be corrected.

## When something does not match

<Steps>
  <Step title="Count or add up again">
    Most differences come from a miscount or a missed slip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for the cause">
    Common causes are a sale entered with the wrong payment method, a split payment entered wrongly, cash taken out without being recorded, or a sale recorded in only one of Loyverse and Shopify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do not fix it by changing the cash">
    Never add or remove money to make the drawer match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a note">
    Write the date, the check that failed, the two amounts, the difference, and what you found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the owner">
    Send the note to the owner the same day. During the trial, Loyverse is the record that counts. See [Source of truth during the trial](/reports-and-management/pos-decision#source-of-truth-during-the-trial).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not refund or redo a sale to fix a payment method. The refund process is still waiting for a decision. Report it to the owner instead.
</Warning>
