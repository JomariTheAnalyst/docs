The [daily closing checks](/reports-and-management/closing-checks) catch problems on the day. These checks catch patterns: the same item marked damaged every week, or a Care of Boss sale nobody paid back.

## Every week

| Check | Where to look | What you are looking for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock lowered as **Damaged**, **Theft or loss**, or **Promotion or donation** | The inventory adjustment history in Shopify admin | Each one had supervisor approval. The same item or the same staff member does not keep coming up |
| Discounts given | The discounts column in [Sixthgear Sales Reporting](/reports-and-management/sales-reports), set to last week | Each discount has a reason, and the total looks normal for the week |
| **Care of Boss** sales not yet paid back | This week's Care of Boss lists from the [closing checks](/reports-and-management/closing-checks), compared with the cash drawer entries "Care of Boss reimbursement, order #SG…" | Every Care of Boss sale has a matching reimbursement. Send the owner a list of the ones still open |
| Any **Custom sale** | The closing check notes for the week | None. If there was one, it was corrected |
| Products missing a business unit or report category | [Sixthgear Sales Reporting](/reports-and-management/sales-reports). Products without both labels show as "Needs fixing" | Nothing under "Needs fixing". See [How products work](/products-and-stock/overview) |

## Every month

| Check | Where to look | What you are looking for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full stock count | Every shelf, using [Count and correct stock](/products-and-stock/count-and-correct-stock) | Shopify's stock matches the shelf for every item |
| Shopify and Loyverse totals for the month, during the trial | Both systems, for the whole month | The totals match. Any difference is explained by a daily closing note. See [Source of truth during the trial](/reports-and-management/pos-decision#source-of-truth-during-the-trial) |
| Prices | The product list in Shopify admin | Prices are still right. Changes follow [Change a price](/products-and-stock/change-a-price) |
| Products no longer sold | The product list in Shopify admin | Products that are no longer sold are archived, so they leave the website and the tablet |

When a check finds something, follow [When something shows up](/reports-and-management/loss-risks#when-something-shows-up).
