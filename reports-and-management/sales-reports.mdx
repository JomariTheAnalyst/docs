SixthGear has two saved reports in Shopify. They show sales from the counter and the website together, unless you filter them.

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sixthgear Sales Reporting** | Sales by business unit and report category |
| **Sixthgear Sales by Product** | Sales for each product |

Business unit and report category are two labels on every product. For the full list, see [Products and Stock](/products-and-stock/overview).

## Open a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Shopify admin">
    Log in with your own account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Analytics, then Reports">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the report name">
    For example, **Sixthgear Sales Reporting**. The report opens with its last saved date range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the date range

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the date range at the top of the report">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the dates">
    Pick a ready-made range such as **Today** or **Last 7 days**, or choose a start and end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the range">
    The report updates. Check the dates shown at the top before you read the totals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export to CSV

A CSV file opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the date range first">
    The export copies only what is on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the ... button">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    The file downloads to your computer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Warning>

## What these reports cannot show

The reports show the total for each category. They cannot split each category into Cash and Non-Cash. For that, use the payment totals in the [daily closing checks](/reports-and-management/closing-checks).

A Google Sheet daily sales report that splits each category into Cash and Non-Cash is planned. It is not ready yet.

## For supervisors: custom queries

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sales by unit and category, last 90 days">
    Use this to rebuild **Sixthgear Sales Reporting** for the last 90 days, with totals.

    ```sql
    FROM sales SHOW gross_sales, discounts, net_sales GROUP BY product.metafields.custom.sixthgear_business_unit, product.metafields.sixthgear.report_category WITH TOTALS SINCE -90d UNTIL today ORDER BY net_sales DESC
    ```
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Today's counter sales only">
    Use this at closing to see only the sales made on the counter tablet today.

    ```sql
    FROM sales SHOW gross_sales, discounts, net_sales WHERE is_pos_sale = true GROUP BY product.metafields.custom.sixthgear_business_unit, product.metafields.sixthgear.report_category WITH TOTALS SINCE today UNTIL today
    ```
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

To run a query, open a new report in **Analytics** > **Reports**, open the query editor, paste the query, and run it. Do not save over the two SixthGear reports.
