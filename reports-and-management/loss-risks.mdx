Most of these happen by mistake: a wrong button, a missed step, a busy counter. The checks below catch mistakes and misuse the same way. When something shows up, find out what happened before deciding anything.

| Risk | How it happens | Where to check in Shopify | What stops it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A fake or old QR PH screenshot | The customer shows a screenshot instead of paying, and the sale is recorded as **QR PH** | The **QR PH** total for the day, compared with the payments in the store's QR PH app | Staff tap **QR PH** only after the money shows in the store's app. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment) |
| A discount without approval | A discount is applied that nobody approved, or at the wrong rate | The discounts column in [Sixthgear Sales Reporting](/reports-and-management/sales-reports), and the reason written on each sale | Every discount needs approval and a reason. [Discounts](/at-the-counter/discounts), [Who can do what](/reports-and-management/who-can-do-what) |
| **Custom sale** | An item is typed in by hand instead of picked from the list, so it has no category | Any sale made with **Custom sale** in the [closing checks](/reports-and-management/closing-checks) | Staff never use it. [Ring up a sale](/at-the-counter/ring-up-a-sale) |
| Cash removed without a record | Cash leaves the drawer and nothing is recorded on the cash drawer screen | The difference between counted and expected cash at closing | Every cash removal is approved and recorded with a reason. [Open and close the register](/at-the-counter/open-and-close-register) |
| **Care of Boss** without approval | A sale is recorded as **Care of Boss** but the owner did not treat anyone | Every **Care of Boss** sale of the day, and the note on each one | Owner approval and a note on every one. The owner checks the list. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment#care-of-boss), [Daily closing checks](/reports-and-management/closing-checks) |
| Stock marked damaged or lost | Stock is lowered with the reason **Damaged**, **Theft or loss**, or **Promotion or donation** | The inventory adjustment history for those three reasons | Supervisor approval before lowering stock for these reasons. [Count and correct stock](/products-and-stock/count-and-correct-stock) |
| **Mark unpaid** | A sale is finished with no money received. The items leave stock and nothing is paid | Orders with an unpaid payment status | Staff never use it. The owner is deciding whether to hide it. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment#mark-unpaid) |
| A sale in only one system during the trial | A sale goes into Loyverse but not Shopify, or the other way round | The Loyverse total compared with the Shopify total at closing | Every sale goes into both. When they disagree, Loyverse counts. [Using Loyverse and Shopify together](/at-the-counter/loyverse-and-shopify), [Source of truth during the trial](/reports-and-management/pos-decision#source-of-truth-during-the-trial) |

## When something shows up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write down what you found">
    The date, the order number, for example SG1042, the amount, and the staff PIN on the sale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the staff member what happened">
    Most answers are a mistake that is easy to explain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the owner the same day">
    The owner decides what to do next. Do not change or refund the sale yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>
