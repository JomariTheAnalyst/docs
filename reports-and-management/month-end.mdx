At the start of each month, export last month's sales from Shopify and send the files to the accountant.

## The exports

| Export | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Sales by business unit and category | The saved report **Sixthgear Sales Reporting** |
| Sales by product | The saved report **Sixthgear Sales by Product** |
| Totals per payment method | Not confirmed yet |
| The month's Care of Boss sales | Not confirmed yet. Until then, use the daily lists from the [closing checks](/reports-and-management/closing-checks) |

How to open a report and export it: [Sales reports in Shopify](/reports-and-management/sales-reports).

## The date range

Use the first day of the month to the last day, for example 1 to 30 November. Set the date range before you export. The export copies only what is on screen.
