This table is the single place for who may do what. Each cell links to the page that sets the rule. If no page sets it, the cell says **Not decided yet** and the owner decides. Update this page first when a rule changes, then the linked page.

| Action | Cashier | Supervisor | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apply a 10%, 15%, or 20% discount | Applies it after the supervisor approves. [Discounts](/at-the-counter/discounts) | Approves it. **Provisional, owner to confirm.** [Discounts](/at-the-counter/discounts) | Not decided yet. [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions) |
| Apply the executive discount | Not decided yet | Cannot approve it. Owner only. [Discounts](/at-the-counter/discounts) | Approves it and sets the amount. [Discounts](/at-the-counter/discounts) |
| Lower a price at checkout, for a hauling destination with a price range | Asks the supervisor. [Services, carwash, and hauling](/at-the-counter/services-carwash-hauling) | Lowers the price on the item before checkout. [Services, carwash, and hauling](/at-the-counter/services-carwash-hauling) | Not decided yet |
| Remove cash from the drawer | Records it after the supervisor approves. [Open and close the register](/at-the-counter/open-and-close-register) | Approves it. [Open and close the register](/at-the-counter/open-and-close-register) | Not decided yet |
| Record a Care of Boss sale | Records it, with a note, after the owner approves. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment#care-of-boss) | Not decided yet | Approves it and pays the store back. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment#care-of-boss) |
| Use **Mark unpaid** | Never. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment#mark-unpaid) | Not decided yet | Not decided yet: hide the button or keep it for owner-only pay-later sales. [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions) |
| Lower stock for **Damaged**, **Theft or loss**, or **Promotion or donation** | Records it after the supervisor approves. [Count and correct stock](/products-and-stock/count-and-correct-stock) | Approves it. [Count and correct stock](/products-and-stock/count-and-correct-stock) | Not decided yet |
| Correct stock after a count (**Count** or **Correction**) | Not decided yet | Not decided yet | Not decided yet |
| Change a product price in Shopify | Changes it only when approved. [Change a price](/products-and-stock/change-a-price) | Approves it. [Change a price](/products-and-stock/change-a-price) | Approves it. [Change a price](/products-and-stock/change-a-price) |
| Add or edit a product (not the price) | Not decided yet | Not decided yet | Not decided yet |
| Edit and publish on the website CMS | Not decided yet: who gets a CMS account | Approves the change. [Edit and publish a change](/website-content/edit-and-publish) | Approves the change. [Edit and publish a change](/website-content/edit-and-publish) |

**Not decided yet** means no page sets the rule. The owner decides.

Refunds, returns, and exchanges are not in this table. The whole process is waiting for a decision and is marked **BLOCKING** on [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions). Until it is decided, nobody gives a refund at the counter.
