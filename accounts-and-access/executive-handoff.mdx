</Info>

## Executive handoff checklist

| Deliverable | Detailed guide | Owner account or system | Evidence required | Status |

## What the credentials inventory records

The inventory records responsibility and access information. It does not store the actual secret.

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Shopify, Sanity, GitHub, Vercel, Mintlify, domain provider, email, social media, or another approved service |
| Account identifier | Company-controlled email address or named account |
| Business owner | Person accountable for the platform |
| Access role | Owner, administrator, editor, developer, billing, or viewer |
| Password location | Approved password-manager vault and item name only |
| MFA custodian | Person or approved device responsible for MFA |
| Backup owner | Authorized backup person |
| Recovery method | Company-controlled recovery email, phone, or platform recovery process |
| Signed-in devices | Approved company devices only |
| Verification date | Date the access was tested |
| Status | Verified, pending, restricted, or remove access |

<Warning>
  Do not record passwords, tokens, MFA seeds, one-time codes, recovery codes, private keys, bank details, or API credentials in this page or any other Mintlify document.
</Warning>

## Handoff completion rule

The turnover is complete only when management can independently open the approved platforms, locate the product export, identify each access owner, and recover access through company-controlled methods.
