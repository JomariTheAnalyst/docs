<Warning>
  This handoff records responsibility, access, and verification. It must never contain actual passwords, tokens, MFA seeds, one-time codes, recovery codes, private keys, or full payment-card details.
</Warning>

## Meeting procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the credentials inventory">
    Confirm that the supplied devices, social platforms, email accounts, business platforms, subscriptions, handlers, phone numbers, and masked payment reference are accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm password and MFA custody">
    Confirm that Stephanie Grace Lelina is responsible for password and MFA custody, and document the approved backup process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Shopify access">
    Ask management to open the correct store and confirm the approved access areas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test SixthGear CMS access">
    Open the correct Sanity project and confirm the assigned role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test source-code access">
    Open the SixthGear GitHub repository and confirm that `main` is visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the product CSV">
    Confirm that management can access and open the latest verified export from controlled company storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record gaps and acceptance">
    List missing invitations, insufficient roles, unavailable recovery methods, or missing backup administrators before signing off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Completion rule

The handoff is complete only when management can independently open the approved systems, locate the product export, identify the responsible people, and use company-controlled recovery methods.
