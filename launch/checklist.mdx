**Opening:** around 22 November 2026. **Not final.**

Tick each task when it is done. Anything that still needs a decision links to [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions) instead of repeating it here.

## Decisions needed first

- [ ] The refund, return, and exchange process is decided. **BLOCKING.** [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions)
- [x] How the five business units are separated in Shopify is decided: 5 business units, 35 report categories, two labels on every product, and two saved reports. [How products work](/products-and-stock/overview)
- [ ] The owner confirms who approves 10%, 15%, and 20% discounts. It is provisional now. [Who can do what](/reports-and-management/who-can-do-what)
- [ ] Every other item on [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions) is answered.

## Shopify setup

- [ ] The tax setting in Shopify matches the store's BIR registration. [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions)
- [ ] The **Care of boss** button is renamed **Care of Boss**. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment#care-of-boss)
- [ ] **Mark unpaid** is hidden or restricted, as the owner decides. [Take payment](/at-the-counter/take-payment#mark-unpaid)
- [ ] Counter-only items are removed from the **Sixthgear Moto** sales channel. [Add a counter-only item](/products-and-stock/add-counter-item)
- [ ] Every product has both a business unit and a report category. [How products work](/products-and-stock/overview)
- [ ] Duplicate **Standard** shipping rates are checked, so customers see one. [How online orders work](/online-orders/overview#shipping-or-store-pickup)
- [ ] The shipping and pickup emails Shopify sends customers are decided. [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions)
- [ ] The website webhook is confirmed working, so product changes reach the website. [Shopify integration](/website/shopify-integration#revalidation)

## At the counter

- [ ] The receipt printer prints a test page from the tablet. [The Shopify POS decision](/reports-and-management/pos-decision#hardware)
- [ ] Every test sale made during setup is tagged `test`. [Sales reports in Shopify](/reports-and-management/sales-reports)
- [ ] Every staff member is trained. [Your first shift](/pos/first-shift), [Open decisions](/reports-and-management/open-decisions)
